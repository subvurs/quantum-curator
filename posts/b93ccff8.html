Planckian Selected for Phase 1 of EuroHPC Quantum Grand Challenge

Source: The Quantum Insider September 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first time an Italian startup has broken into the EuroHPC Quantum Grand Challenge, a flagship EU effort to accelerate quantum technologies with a clear industrial‑scale roadmap. Being one of only thirteen firms chosen from more than two dozen proposals signals that Planckian’s hardware approach is viewed as credible alongside other European heavyweights such as QuTech and IQM. The Phase 1 award will give the company access to pan‑European supercomputing resources and funding, potentially speeding up prototype development and ecosystem integration. However, the program remains in its early stages, so commercial impact will depend on how quickly Planckian can translate this support into a scalable quantum processor.

— Mark Eatherly
