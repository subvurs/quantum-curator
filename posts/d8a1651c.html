Fortaegis Raises $50 Million to Commercialize Chip-Based Security Technology

Source: The Quantum Insider September 14, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Fortaegis is scaling a hardware‑level security platform that could become a critical layer of protection for the high‑bandwidth, low‑latency networks increasingly used to link AI workloads, autonomous platforms and satellite constellations—systems that are prime targets for both classical cyber attacks and future quantum threats. The $50 million Series A follows a wave of post‑quantum cryptography initiatives and hardware‑rooted trust solutions, signaling investor confidence that chip‑based defenses will be essential as quantum computers move closer to practical key‑breaking capabilities. If the company can deliver mass‑produced, tamper‑resistant chips, it could give defense and commercial AI operators a ready‑to‑deploy safeguard while buying time for broader quantum‑secure protocols to mature.

— Mark Eatherly
