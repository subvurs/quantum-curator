EuroHPC Opens €119M in Funding Calls for Quantum Technologies

Source: The Quantum Insider August 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This €119 million EuroHPC initiative marks the most coordinated European push yet to build a full‑stack trapped‑ion quantum ecosystem, signaling that Europe is moving from isolated research projects toward industrial‑scale development. By funding both hardware platforms and the supporting infrastructure, the calls aim to close the gap with U.S. and Chinese roadmaps and give European startups and consortia the resources needed to prototype fault‑tolerant processors before 2030. While the funding is substantial, success will still depend on delivering scalable ion‑trap architectures and integrating them into existing HPC workflows, making the next few years a critical test of Europe’s quantum ambition.

— Mark Eatherly
