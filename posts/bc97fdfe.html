Source: The Quantum Insider July 31, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first full‑scale commercial rollouts of a silicon‑photonic quantum security platform, moving Miraex’s laboratory‑grade photonic chips into real‑world PKI and encryption products. It builds on recent momentum from QKD network deployments and IBM’s roadmap for photonic processors, showing that hardware manufacturers are now bundling quantum‑ready modules with existing semiconductor lines rather than treating them as niche research tools. If SEALSQ can deliver reliable, high‑volume photonic chips, enterprises could start upgrading to post‑quantum cryptography without a complete infrastructure overhaul, though scaling yields and industry standards remain key hurdles.
