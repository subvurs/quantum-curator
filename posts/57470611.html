IonQ | IonQ Achieves Record 1 kHz Quantum Interconnect Speed

Source: IonQ Blog October 09, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights IonQ’s breakthrough in reaching a 1 kHz quantum interconnect rate between a trapped‑ion processor and a dedicated quantum memory—a tenfold jump over the tens‑of‑hertz links that have dominated recent experiments. By demonstrating fast, low‑latency photon‑mediated coupling, the work directly supports modular architectures that aim to scale beyond a few dozen ions while preserving coherence for error‑corrected operations. Although 1 kHz is still far from the gigahertz bandwidth needed for large‑scale networks, the result marks a concrete milestone toward practical distributed quantum computers and shows that trapped‑ion platforms can keep pace with competing hardware approaches.

— Mark Eatherly
