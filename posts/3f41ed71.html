Türkiye Signs Agreement with IQM Quantum Computers to Deploy 54-Qubit Radiance R3 System at TÜBİTAK in 2027

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks Turkey’s first foray into mid‑scale superconducting quantum hardware, moving from a five‑qubit prototype to IQM’s 54‑qubit Radiance R3 platform that will sit alongside its national HPC resources by 2027. The deployment gives Turkish academia and industry sovereign access to a machine capable of running error‑mitigated algorithms and benchmarking against the global race toward fault‑tolerant processors, echoing similar national initiatives in Europe and Asia. While still far from practical advantage over classical supercomputers, the system will accelerate local talent development, software stack integration, and collaborative experiments that could feed into larger international quantum roadmaps.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Türkiye's Scientific and Technological Research Council (TÜBİTAK) has partnered with IQM Quantum Computers to deploy a 54-qubit superconducting quantum computer, the IQM Radiance R3 system, in 2027. This system will provide sovereign quantum computing capacity for Turkish universities, research institutes, and private sectors, integrating with TÜBİTAK's existing high-performance computing environment. The deployment signifies an expansion of Türkiye's quantum infrastructure, following the 2024 unveiling of the five-qubit QuanT processor.
