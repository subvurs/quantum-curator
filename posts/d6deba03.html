Telia, Nokia And QMill Demonstrate Quantum-Powered Encryption in a 5G Network

Source: The Quantum Insider November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first time a quantum‑enhanced encryption scheme has been run on commercial 5G hardware, showing that post‑quantum security can be layered onto existing telecom infrastructure without redesigning the network stack. By embedding QMill’s protocol into Nokia’s base‑station software and testing it in Telia’s live‑network sandbox, the demonstration bridges the gap between academic cryptography and real‑world deployment, echoing recent industry moves toward quantum‑resistant standards such as NIST’s PQC suite. While still a proof‑of‑concept limited to an innovation environment, the work signals that operators can begin hardening their services now rather than waiting for large‑scale quantum computers to appear.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Insider Brief PRESS RELEASE — Telia Finland, Nokia and QMill have demonstrated the world’s first quantum-powered encryption in a 5G network using commercial equipment. In the proof of concept (PoC), QMill’s solution was integrated into Nokia’s 5G base station software and successfully demonstrated in Telia’s 5G innovation environment.
