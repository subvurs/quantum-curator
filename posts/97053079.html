Mountain West Groups Partner to Support Quantum and Photonics Innovation

Source: The Quantum Insider August 14, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it formalises a regional network that will give quantum‑hardware developers and photonics researchers access to shared testbeds and fabrication resources far beyond the traditional coastal hubs. By linking the NSF ASCEND Engine’s Innosphere program with the Montana Photonics and Quantum Alliance and Headwaters Tech Hub, the Mountain West can become a practical proving ground for scalable qubit architectures and integrated photonic interconnects, complementing similar efforts in Europe’s quantum flagship and the U.S. national labs. The partnership promises faster iteration cycles for startups and university teams, though its impact will hinge on sustained funding and the ability to integrate disparate facilities into a coherent ecosystem.

— Mark Eatherly
