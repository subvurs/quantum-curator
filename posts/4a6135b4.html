Congressman Langworthy Introduces American Quantum Competitiveness Act (H.R. 10163)

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 01, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it signals a concrete step toward consolidating U.S. quantum‑technology policy under the Department of Commerce, giving the government a clear point person for funding, supply‑chain resilience and commercial rollout. It builds on the momentum of the National Quantum Initiative Act and recent bipartisan bills aimed at keeping America competitive with China’s aggressive quantum investments. If enacted, the legislation could streamline grants and procurement for startups and manufacturers, though its impact will hinge on appropriations and effective inter‑agency coordination.

— Mark Eatherly
