Summary

Despite an expanded operating loss due to increased R&D, the company maintains a strong liquidity position with $286.5 million in cash and significant funding commitments. Operationally, Xanadu is focused on reducing photonic error rates through industrial partnerships to advance its logical qubit roadmap.
