Entanglement cost and classical capacity of multimode Gaussian states and channels

Source: arXiv Quantum Physics November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article settles a long‑standing open problem by proving that for any finite‑energy multimode Gaussian state the entanglement of formation, its Gaussian variant and the operational entanglement cost all coincide, giving a single, strongly superadditive figure of merit that also lower‑bounds non‑Gaussian states with the same covariance matrix. By extending the result to multimode Gaussian channels, the authors obtain exact formulas for both the entanglement cost and the classical capacity under realistic energy constraints, showing that pure Gaussian inputs with simple displacement modulation are already optimal—a finding that streamlines design of continuous‑variable quantum communication systems such as CV‑QKD networks. The work builds on recent advances in bosonic channel capacity theory and resource‑theoretic approaches, while its applicability is limited to finite‑energy regimes, leaving the infinite‑energy idealizations for future study.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Gaussian states and operations are central to continuous-variable quantum information and communication. For systems with any finite number of modes, we prove that entanglement of formation, Gaussian entanglement of formation, and entanglement cost coincide for every finite-energy bipartite Gaussian state. Their common value is strongly superadditive and lower-bounds entanglement of formation and entanglement cost for arbitrary non-Gaussian states with the same covariance matrix. We then determine the entanglement cost of every multimode Gaussian channel as well as its classical capacity under a mean energy constraint. These quantities reduce to Gaussian covariance optimizations, and the capacity is attained by pure Gaussian signals with Gaussian displacement modulation, independently across channel uses. The proof combines geodesic convexity of entanglement for pure Gaussian states with a relative-entropy inequality for arbitrary states. We also obtain a closed formula for the entanglement cost of a family of four-mode bound entangled states.
