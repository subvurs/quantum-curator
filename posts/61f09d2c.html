Classical Light Dynamics Could Help Simulate Quantum Interactions

Source: The Quantum Insider November 13, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows that multimode nonlinear optical systems—essentially classical light setups—can be engineered to generate effective photon‑photon interactions that mimic quantum many‑body dynamics and even drive thermalization. By leveraging well‑understood photonic hardware, the approach offers a potentially lower‑cost analog platform for exploring complex quantum phenomena such as entanglement growth and phase transitions, complementing recent advances in programmable photonic processors and boson‑sampling experiments. However, practical deployment will still hinge on controlling loss and nonlinear response with sufficient precision to faithfully reproduce the target quantum Hamiltonians.

— Mark Eatherly
