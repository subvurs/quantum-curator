U.S. Quantum Ambitions Face a Supply Chain Test, Policy Analysts Warn

Source: The Quantum Insider November 13, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article flags a critical choke point: without a robust domestic supply chain for specialized lasers, cryogenic systems and low‑loss photonic components, the United States risks turning its quantum research breakthroughs into commercial dead ends. It echoes recent warnings from industry leaders that Europe’s “Quantum Flagship” and China’s state‑backed chip programs are already securing manufacturing capacity, putting U.S. firms at a competitive disadvantage. By urging targeted federal investment in component fabrication and workforce development, the analysts highlight a policy lever that could accelerate the transition from laboratory prototypes to market‑ready quantum processors.

— Mark Eatherly
