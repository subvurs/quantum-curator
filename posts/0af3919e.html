NEC Halts Development of Quantum Computer Hardware

Source: The Quantum Insider September 06, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because NEC’s withdrawal underscores how even well‑funded, legacy players still find the engineering and economic hurdles of building a scalable quantum processor daunting. It comes at a time when industry giants such as IBM, Google and emerging startups are racing to demonstrate error‑corrected qubits, highlighting that hardware breakthroughs remain scarce and costly. The move may accelerate NEC’s shift toward quantum software or strategic partnerships, but it also serves as a reminder that commercial quantum computers are likely still several years away for most applications.
