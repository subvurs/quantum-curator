Researchers Show Sunlight Can Generate Quantum Entanglement

Source: The Quantum Insider August 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows that natural sunlight can serve as a source of entangled photons, overturning the prevailing view that only coherent laser light is viable for quantum‑state preparation. By demonstrating entanglement with an abundant, free energy source, the work dovetails with recent efforts to build low‑power quantum sensors and satellite‑based quantum communication links that must operate outside laboratory conditions. If scalable, sunlight‑driven entanglement could dramatically reduce the energy footprint of quantum networks, though challenges remain in filtering and stabilizing the broadband solar spectrum for reliable protocols.

— Mark Eatherly
