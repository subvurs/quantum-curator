T-SQUARED Begins Construction on Infleqtion’s Quantum Innovation Centre in Oxford

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 13, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a tangible step toward scaling neutral‑atom quantum technology, as Infleqtion’s new Oxford centre will triple its UK capacity for research, chip fabrication, and system integration. By consolidating quantum computing, sensing and precision‑timing work under one roof, the facility mirrors recent moves by companies such as QuEra and Pasqal to move from prototype labs into manufacturable platforms. The expansion signals growing confidence in neutral‑atom architectures, though commercial impact will still depend on overcoming yield and error‑rate challenges before large‑scale deployment.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Specialized engineering firm T-SQUARED has commenced construction on a new Quantum Innovation Centre at Oxford Technology Park for neutral-atom quantum technology company Infleqtion (NYSE: INFQ). The facility will triple the capacity of Infleqtion’s UK operations, expanding its research, manufacturing, and systems integration infrastructure for neutral-atom quantum computing, quantum sensing, and precision timing platforms.
