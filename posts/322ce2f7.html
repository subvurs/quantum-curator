A Unified Protocol for Arbitrary Z Rotations of a Class of Bosonic cQED Qubits

Source: arXiv Quantum Physics October 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it offers the first systematic recipe that works for arbitrary Z‑rotations on a whole family of bosonic Fock‑based qubits rather than requiring bespoke pulse sequences for each encoding. By distilling the common structure into a six‑step protocol, the work streamlines calibration and could accelerate the integration of error‑corrected bosonic logical gates alongside recent advances in cat‑code and binomial‑code hardware. The result is a practical design rule—if an encoding satisfies the identified condition, developers can reuse the same control stack, lowering engineering overhead while still preserving the high coherence that makes bosonic qubits attractive.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Bosonic-qubit encodings provide diverse approaches to storing and processing quantum information, with logical operations often implemented through encoding-specific physical strategies. Here, we identify a class of bosonic Fock-based qubits in circuit QED for which distinct implementations of arbitrary Z rotations share a common structure. We formulate this structure as a six-step protocol that provides a unified procedure for implementing arbitrary Z rotations across this class of bosonic cQED qubits. The applicability of the protocol is determined by a specific condition on the encoding, providing a criterion for identifying bosonic Fock-based cQED qubits that admit the common implementation.
