AI Commentary

This article reveals that commercial quantum users are already stretching the limits of modest‑size NISQ processors, submitting circuits that average six qubits while gravitating toward the most cost‑effective hardware available. The trend signals a market split between developers who need larger logical depths for algorithmic research and enterprises that prioritize price and uptime over raw performance—a pattern echoed in recent IBM Qiskit usage reports and Rigetti’s cloud‑access growth. By quantifying how pricing, speed and error rates drive workload placement, the study gives hardware vendors concrete data to shape tiered service offerings and helps investors gauge when demand will shift toward higher‑fidelity, larger‑scale machines.
