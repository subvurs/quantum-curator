This article marks a concrete step toward fault‑tolerant quantum computers by showing that Google’s Willow chip can distill high‑fidelity T‑states with a 40× error suppression and logical fidelity of roughly 99.99 %, dramatically shrinking the number of physical qubits needed for magic‑state factories. Summary

Google Quantum AI has successfully demonstrated magic state cultivation on its Willow quantum processor, achieving a 40-fold error reduction and producing T-states with 99.99(1)% logical fidelity. This breakthrough addresses a major bottleneck in fault-tolerant quantum computing by significantly reducing the physical qubit footprint required for generating these essential states, directly validating Google's 2024 cultivation theory framework.
