Black Kite Adds Quantum Readiness Checks for Third-Party Vendors

Source: The Quantum Insider October 22, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows that cyber‑risk vendors are beginning to embed post‑quantum considerations into supply‑chain assessments, signaling that the industry takes the looming threat of quantum attacks seriously. By adding a “Quantum Readiness” control to its SSL/TLS rating, Black Kite gives enterprises a concrete way to spot which third‑party partners have already adopted or can quickly transition to quantum‑resistant cryptography—a move that dovetails with NIST’s ongoing post‑quantum standards rollout. The practical payoff is an early warning system for organizations to prioritize TLS upgrades across their vendor ecosystem, though the actual risk of a large‑scale quantum break remains several years away and readiness will still hinge on concrete algorithm migrations.

— Mark Eatherly
