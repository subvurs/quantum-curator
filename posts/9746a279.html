Quantum Residual Exploration for Selected Configuration Interaction

Source: arXiv Quantum Physics November 09, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article introduces a hybrid quantum‑classical workflow that lets a trapped‑ion processor suggest new determinants for selected configuration interaction, accepting only those that improve the variational energy relative to purely classical candidates. By targeting multistep excitations that perturbative schemes often miss, it demonstrates a measurable energy gain for a stretched H₁₂ chain while keeping the total determinant count fixed—a clear step toward more efficient quantum‑assisted electronic‑structure methods. The result shows how modest near‑term devices can augment traditional algorithms, though scaling to larger molecules will still require advances in error mitigation and hardware connectivity.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

We propose a modular hybrid quantum-classical method to improve selected configuration interaction at a fixed final determinant budget. Quantum measurements supply complementary determinants, accepted only when they outrank classical candidates under a common energetic criterion. This targets multistep excitations that perturbative discovery may miss in strongly correlated systems. A demonstration on the Quantinuum Reimei trapped-ion quantum computer shows that measured proposals for H$_{12}$ at a stretched geometry lower the variational energy relative to classical-only selection at the same final determinant budget.
