IQM Quantum Computers Sells 54-Qubit Radiance R3 System to South Korean Quantum-AI Infrastructure Firm QAI

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This deal marks IQM’s first private‑sector deployment of its Radiance R3 hardware in Asia, signaling that mid‑scale superconducting processors are moving beyond research labs into commercial data centers. By pairing a 54‑qubit system with QAI’s Quantum‑as‑a‑Service platform, the agreement dovetails with South Korea’s national quantum strategy and could accelerate AI‑driven applications that need near‑term quantum acceleration. It also underscores a broader industry trend of vendors targeting cloud‑style access models rather than selling machines outright, though the modest qubit count still limits execution to error‑mitigated prototypes rather than full‑scale fault‑tolerant workloads.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

IQM Quantum Computers sold a 54-qubit Radiance R3 system to QAI Co., Ltd. in South Korea for EUR 8-10 million. The quantum computer will be installed in QAI's AI data center in Seoul by the end of 2027, making it IQM's first deployment in a private commercial data center in Asia. QAI will offer Quantum-as-a-Service to various organizations, supporting South Korea's national quantum strategy.
