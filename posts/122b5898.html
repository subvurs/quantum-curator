QuTech Demonstrates Coherent Control of a Majorana-Based Qubit in Coupled Kitaev Chains

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first demonstration of coherent Rabi driving on a Majorana‑based qubit realized in coupled Kitaev chains, showing that topological modes can be manipulated at microwave frequencies (≈115 MHz) in an indium antimonide nanowire. It builds on recent strides toward topological quantum hardware from groups such as Microsoft and Delft, moving the field beyond static signatures of Majoranas to actual gate‑level control—a key step toward intrinsically fault‑tolerant qubits. While coherence times remain short, extending the chains promises reduced decoherence and paves the way for more complex braiding or error‑corrected operations that could dramatically lower overhead in future quantum processors.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Researchers at QuTech have successfully demonstrated coherent control of a Majorana-based qubit using coupled Kitaev chains. This qubit, fabricated in an indium antimonide nanowire, operates at 20 mK and exhibits Rabi frequencies of approximately 113-117 MHz. While current coherence times are limited, the team is working on extending the platform to longer chains to improve decoherence and enable advanced operations.
