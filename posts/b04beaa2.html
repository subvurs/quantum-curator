This article highlights the expanding toolbox of solid‑state qubits, showing that quantum dots and diamond nitrogen‑vacancy centers are moving from niche experiments toward commercially viable platforms. The coverage also underscores how multiple companies are racing to mature each technology, meaning the field will benefit from healthy competition and a broader set of engineering trade‑offs for future applications.
