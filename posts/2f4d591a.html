Ellis: Extremely Fast Quantum Circuit Simulation under Qubit Loss and Leakage

Source: arXiv Quantum Physics November 19, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article introduces Ellis, a simulator that can model Clifford‑type fault‑tolerant circuits even when qubits are lost or leak, something traditional Pauli‑frame methods could not handle because the circuit varies shot‑by‑shot. By identifying “frame‑CSS” circuits that retain a common reference despite loss, the authors achieve near‑linear‑time certification and a staggering 952 k shots per second on a single core—orders of magnitude faster than existing tools such as Clifft or PPVM. The breakthrough opens the door to realistic large‑scale testing of error‑corrected quantum algorithms under hardware‑relevant loss and leakage, though its applicability is currently limited to circuits that fit the frame‑CSS structure.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Qubit loss and leakage are persistent errors: while a qubit remains lost or leaked, every operation acting on it is dropped. Consequently, the Clifford circuit executed differs from shot to shot. This variation breaks Pauli-frame simulation, which constructs each shot by XORing sampled Pauli flips onto a single \emph{common reference} measurement record. By rethinking the roles of tableau simulation and Pauli-frame simulation, we show that many key fault-tolerant primitives nevertheless retain a common reference under loss. These include CSS code memory, lattice surgery, and transversal CNOT. Such circuits can therefore be simulated exactly under Pauli noise and loss by a Pauli frame with per-shot masks for the dropped operations. We define \emph{frame-CSS} circuits as those that are CSS up to local single-qubit basis changes, given a loss model that specifies where qubits can be lost and reloaded. We prove that every frame-CSS circuit has a common reference, and we give a practically near-linear-time procedure that certifies whether a circuit is frame-CSS. We implement the certifier and a high-performance simulator in the open-source package \href{https://github.com/isaachyw/ELLIS}{\textsc{Ellis}} (Efficient Loss and Leakage Invariant-reference Simulation). On the gross code under qubit loss and Pauli errors, \ellis{} samples 952{,}000 shots per second on a single CPU core, at least $1000\times$ faster than Clifft~\cite{chase2026} and PPVM~\cite{luo2026ppvm}.
