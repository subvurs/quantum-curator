Beijing Launches Program to Connect Quantum Companies With Industrial Customers

Source: The Quantum Insider September 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

AI Commentary

This article highlights Beijing’s new “Scenario Handshake Plan,” a government‑backed effort to pair quantum startups and university labs with end‑users in transportation, energy, finance and other sectors, signaling a decisive move from pure research toward market‑ready solutions. By creating a structured matchmaking platform, China aims to accelerate real‑world pilots that can validate quantum advantage while giving companies clearer pathways to revenue—a strategy echoing similar industry‑focused programs emerging in the US and Europe. The initiative’s impact will hinge on how quickly hardware performance improves enough to meet the demanding use cases of these heavyweight industries.
