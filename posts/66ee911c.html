Responsible Fintech Institute and Safeheron Launch Cross-Regional Post-Quantum Digital Asset Pilot

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first coordinated, cross‑border trials of post‑quantum cryptography applied to real‑world digital‑asset wallets, showing how the financial sector is moving from theoretical standards to operational security. By bringing together commercial banks, regulators and a specialized security provider, the pilot builds on recent NIST PQC standardization efforts and parallels other industry experiments such as IBM’s quantum‑safe networking trials, helping align technical solutions with emerging policy frameworks. While promising, the work remains confined to a controlled environment, so broader adoption will still depend on scaling the technology and achieving regulatory consensus across jurisdictions.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

The Responsible Fintech Institute (RFI) and digital asset security provider Safeheron have launched a cross-border post-quantum cryptography (PQC) pilot to evaluate quantum-resilient digital asset transaction infrastructure. The initiative convenes international commercial banks and financial regulators across multiple jurisdictions to test post-quantum wallet generation and transaction signing in a controlled environment.
