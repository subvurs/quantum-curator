Alice & Bob Accelerates Modular Cat-Qubit Roadmap to 200 Logical Qubits from 3,600 Physical Qubits by 2030

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 18, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article is noteworthy because it shows that Alice & Bob’s cat‑qubit platform can shrink the physical‑to‑logical qubit overhead to roughly 18:1, a ratio far better than most superconducting or trapped‑ion approaches and therefore a realistic path toward hundreds of error‑corrected qubits within the next decade. By leveraging modular hardware and AI‑driven layout tools, the roadmap sidesteps the traditional bottleneck of scaling a single dilution refrigerator, positioning the Carbon system for near‑term quantum chemistry and cryptographic simulations that were previously out of reach. The timeline remains ambitious—achieving 200 logical qubits by 2030 will still require sustained advances in cat‑qubit coherence, inter‑module connectivity, and fault‑tolerant control software—but the announced milestones mark a concrete step toward practical, large‑scale quantum advantage.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Alice & Bob has unveiled an accelerated quantum computing roadmap, aiming for 200 logical qubits from 3,600 physical cat qubits by 2030 in their Carbon system, and scaling to 2,048 logical qubits by 2034. This advancement is due to their cat qubits' ability to suppress bit-flip errors, reducing the physical-to-logical qubit ratio. The modular architecture and AI-assisted design contribute to bypassing single-fridge scaling limits, enabling complex simulations and cryptographic applications.
