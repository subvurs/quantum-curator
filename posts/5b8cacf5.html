Wolfspeed Introduces Optical-Grade Silicon Carbide for Augmented Reality and Quantum Computing

Source: The Quantum Insider October 30, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Wolfspeed’s transparent silicon‑carbide (TSiC™) offers a rare combination of wide bandgap robustness and optical transparency, opening a path to monolithic photonic circuits that can route control light directly on cryogenic quantum chips. By leveraging SiC’s established high‑power and low‑loss properties, the material could simplify the bulky fiber‑to‑chip interfaces that currently limit scaling of superconducting and spin‑based qubits, while also aligning with industry moves toward integrated silicon‑photonic quantum processors seen from IBM, Google and emerging startups. The breakthrough is promising but will still require demonstrated low‑noise waveguide performance and reliable integration with existing qubit architectures before it can become a mainstream component in quantum hardware stacks.

— Mark Eatherly
