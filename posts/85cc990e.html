IonQ Subsidiary Skyloom Reaches 84 On-Orbit Optical Terminals Supporting SDA Constellation

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This deployment marks a concrete step toward the space‑based quantum network that many researchers have been envisioning, as IonQ’s own trapped‑ion expertise now backs an expanding fleet of optical communication terminals capable of high‑rate, low‑latency links between ground stations. By integrating these OCTs into the SDA Proliferated Warfighter constellation, Skyloom is creating a backbone that could eventually carry quantum‑encrypted data or even entangled photons for secure, global quantum key distribution—a capability already demonstrated in smaller satellite experiments such as China’s Micius mission. While the terminals themselves are not yet quantum repeaters, their presence accelerates the infrastructure needed to link future ground‑based quantum processors and brings a practical, defense‑driven use case closer to reality.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Space-based optical communications developer Skyloom Global—a subsidiary of IonQ (NYSE: IONQ)—has deployed dozens of additional Optical Communication Terminals (OCTs) into low-Earth orbit (LEO).
