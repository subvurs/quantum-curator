Quantum eMotion Completes C$32 Million ($22.5 Million USD) Acquisition of Cybersecurity Provider Plurilock Security

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks one of the first sizable deals that directly couples a quantum‑ready cryptography firm with an established identity‑and‑cloud security provider, signaling that the industry is moving beyond research prototypes toward commercial quantum‑resistant solutions. By folding Quantum eMotion’s QKD and post‑quantum key‑exchange tech into Plurilock’s authentication platform, the combined company can offer NATO‑grade protection for critical infrastructure now, while positioning itself for a future where classical encryption may be compromised. The acquisition also underscores a broader trend of traditional cybersecurity players seeking quantum expertise to stay ahead of looming cryptographic risks, though real‑world deployment will still depend on scaling quantum hardware and seamless integration with existing IT stacks.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantum eMotion Corp. has completed its C$31.98 million acquisition of Plurilock Security Inc., a Canadian cybersecurity company. This strategic move combines Quantum eMotion's quantum cryptography with Plurilock's cybersecurity operations, enhancing their offerings in critical services, identity management, and cloud security across North America and NATO. Plurilock's shares are set to delist from the TSX Venture Exchange around November 26.
