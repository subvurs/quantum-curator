Diraq Installs Silicon Spin Quantum Computer in Equinix Commercial Data Center

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 11, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first time a silicon‑spin quantum processor has been installed outside a research lab, demonstrating that Diraq’s eight‑qubit chip can operate in a standard Equinix data center environment. By proving hardware stability under real‑world cooling and power constraints, the deployment paves the way for scaling to the announced 256‑qubit system and for integrating quantum accelerators into existing cloud infrastructure. If the “Quantum at Work” simulations live up to their promise, such on‑premise quantum nodes could start shaving months off drug‑discovery cycles, though performance and error‑rate benchmarks will still need to match academic prototypes before commercial adoption becomes routine.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Diraq has installed an eight-qubit silicon quantum computer in an Equinix commercial data center in Sydney, Australia, to test its viability outside of lab environments. This deployment aims to assess hardware performance in a real-world data center setting, with plans to upgrade to a 256-qubit processor soon. Diraq's "Quantum at Work" white paper suggests quantum simulation could significantly reduce drug development costs.
