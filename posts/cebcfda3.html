Quandela And Quanova Partner to Deploy Vietnam’s First Quantum Computer

Source: The Quantum Insider October 23, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the deployment of Vietnam’s first quantum computer, highlighting the rapid geographic diversification of photonic‑based hardware beyond its traditional strongholds in Europe and North America. By installing Quandela’s MosaiQ platform at Quang Trung University, the partnership gives Southeast Asian researchers direct access to a scalable, room‑temperature architecture that complements recent advances from Xanadu and PsiQuantum, potentially accelerating regional talent development and industry collaborations. While the installation is a significant step for quantum education and early‑stage experimentation, it remains an exploratory research tool rather than a machine ready for fault‑tolerant applications.

— Mark Eatherly
