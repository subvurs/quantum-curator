RFI and Safeheron Test Post-Quantum Cryptography for Digital Asset Transactions

Source: The Quantum Insider August 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first coordinated pilots that moves post‑quantum cryptography from theory into real‑world digital‑asset workflows, giving banks and regulators a chance to see how NIST‑standardized algorithms perform under production conditions. By involving multiple jurisdictions and financial players, the RFI‑Safeheron effort builds the cross‑border trust framework that will be essential once quantum computers become capable of breaking today’s ECC and RSA keys. The initiative also highlights practical hurdles—such as latency, key‑management integration, and regulatory alignment—that must be solved before quantum‑safe finance can scale.
