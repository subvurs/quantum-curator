Extending qubit lifetime beyond break-even and enhancing quantum sensing by autonomous quantum error correction

Source: Nature Physics November 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first experimental demonstration that an autonomous, measurement‑free error‑correction loop can push a bosonic logical qubit’s coherence past the break‑even point set by its best physical constituent, a milestone that moves fault‑tolerant hardware from theory toward practice. By leveraging engineered dissipation to continuously correct photon loss without interrupting the quantum state, the work builds on recent surface‑code and cat‑code breakthroughs while offering a scalable pathway for protecting more complex logical registers. The same error‑resilient dynamics also translate into higher‑precision quantum sensing, suggesting that near‑term metrological devices could benefit from built‑in error correction before full‑blown fault tolerance is achieved.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Nature Physics, Published online: 16 November 2026; doi:10.1038/s41567-026-03493-z A measurement-free quantum error-correction protocol is shown to extend the lifetime of a bosonic logical qubit beyond the best physical qubit in the same system. This also offers prospects for enhanced quantum sensing.
