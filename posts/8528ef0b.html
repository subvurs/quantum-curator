Researchers Develop Quantum Memories for Long-Distance Networks

Source: The Quantum Insider August 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because reliable quantum memories are the missing link that will let entanglement survive long enough for repeaters to stitch together continental‑scale quantum links, turning today’s laboratory demos into a true quantum internet. The AL FreSQO effort builds on recent breakthroughs in atom‑light interfaces and satellite QKD, showing how Europe is moving from point‑to‑point experiments toward an interoperable, free‑space network backed by EU policy funding. While the project promises longer storage times and higher retrieval efficiencies, scaling those devices into rugged, field‑deployable nodes will still be a key engineering hurdle.

— Mark Eatherly
