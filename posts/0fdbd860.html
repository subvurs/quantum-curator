OTI Lumionics and Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology Achieve 200+ Logical Qubit Quantum Emulation on Single Server Hardware

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 18, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows that OTI Lumionics’s iQCC algorithm can be classically emulated at the scale of more than 200 logical qubits on a single server, providing a rare benchmark for how far quantum‑chemistry workloads could stretch once fault‑tolerant hardware arrives. By reproducing accurate triplet states of Ir(III) and Pt(II) complexes, the work bridges recent advances in noisy‑intermediate‑scale quantum (NISQ) chemistry with the emerging need for error‑corrected simulations that can tackle transition‑metal catalysis. The result gives researchers a concrete performance target for both hardware roadmaps—such as IBM’s 1,000‑qubit plans—and software stacks, while reminding readers that the current achievement is still a classical emulation rather than a physical quantum processor run.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantum software and materials discovery company OTI Lumionics, in collaboration with the Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology (SAIT), has published research benchmarking its proprietary Iterative Qubit Coupled Cluster (iQCC) algorithm in the Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS).
