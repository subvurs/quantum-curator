This article matters because Q.security’s $11.5 million seed round signals the first wave of venture‑backed startups moving beyond research to deliver end‑to‑end post‑quantum cryptography (PQC) solutions for enterprises, a market that has accelerated after NIST’s recent PQC standard selections. By offering a “discovery‑to‑remediation” platform, Q.security aims to close the gap between identifying vulnerable legacy algorithms and deploying quantum‑resistant replacements—a pain point highlighted in recent industry surveys of financial services and cloud providers. The involvement of Coinbase Ventures and New York Life Ventures underscores growing confidence that practical PQC adoption will be a critical security layer for both crypto‑centric firms and traditional businesses alike, though the company will still need to prove scalability across diverse legacy environments.
