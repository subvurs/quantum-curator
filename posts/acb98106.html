Quantinuum Demonstrates 99.9995% Logical Memory Fidelity on Helios Trapped-Ion Processor

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 19, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a concrete step toward fault‑tolerant quantum computing by showing that Quantinuum’s Helios trapped‑ion processor can store quantum information with a logical fidelity of 99.9995 %, corresponding to a per‑round error rate of just 5 × 10⁻⁶ on a distance‑7 surface code. The exponential suppression factor of 12 demonstrates that scaling the code distance continues to yield dramatic improvements, echoing recent advances from superconducting platforms and confirming that trapped ions can meet the stringent thresholds required for large‑scale architectures such as Quantinuum’s upcoming Apollo system. For practitioners, the result suggests that logical memory—once a major bottleneck—may soon be reliable enough to support deeper algorithms without overwhelming overhead, though extending this performance to full logical gates and multi‑qubit operations remains an open challenge.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantinuum has demonstrated a 99.9995% logical memory fidelity on its Helios trapped-ion quantum computer, achieving a logical error rate of 5 × 10-6 per syndrome-extraction round on a distance-7 code. This performance shows exponential error suppression with increasing code distance, with a suppression factor of 12. These results align with the performance targets for Quantinuum's future universal fault-tolerant quantum system, Apollo.
