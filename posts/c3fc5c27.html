University of Sydney Demonstrates Multi-Qubit Logical Entanglement and 170-Round Stability on IBM Square-Lattice Nighthawk r2 Processor

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows the first time a multi‑qubit logical Bell state and three‑logical‑qubit teleportation have been realized on IBM’s square‑lattice Nighthawk r2 chip, demonstrating that error‑corrected operations can be kept coherent for 170 successive syndrome cycles. The dynamic “walking” syndrome extraction circuit builds on recent surface‑code advances and pushes the depth at which logical qubits outperform their physical counterparts, a key milestone for fault‑tolerant architectures. While still far from full algorithmic execution, the result narrows the gap between laboratory demonstrations and the error budgets required for practical quantum advantage.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

University of Sydney researchers have demonstrated significant advancements in quantum error correction on IBM Quantum's ibm_phoenix processor. They achieved multi-qubit fault-tolerant logic and sustained error suppression for up to 170 rounds using a novel dynamic "walking" syndrome circuit. This breakthrough, utilizing the Nighthawk r2 architecture, includes successful logical Bell-state preparation and three-logical-qubit teleportation, marking a crucial step towards scalable fault-tolerant quantum computing.
