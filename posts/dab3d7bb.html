Source: The Quantum Insider July 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first end‑to‑end demonstrations that NIST‑standardized post‑quantum cryptography can be embedded in a safety‑critical robotics stack without breaking real‑time communication with cloud controllers. By pairing Terra Quantum’s expertise in quantum‑resistant algorithms with Apex.AI’s certified automotive software, the collaboration shows how autonomous vehicles and industrial robots can start hardening themselves against future quantum attacks while still meeting existing functional‑safety requirements. The work also highlights practical hurdles—such as latency and certification overhead—that will need to be solved before PQC becomes routine in safety‑critical systems.
