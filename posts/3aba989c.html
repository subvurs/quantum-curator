Source: The Quantum Insider November 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it signals a coordinated effort by a major Korean province to fuse the rapidly converging quantum‑computing and semiconductor sectors, creating an ecosystem where chip‑scale quantum devices can move from lab prototypes to manufacturable products. By bringing together government, academia, and industry leaders such as Samsung and SK Hynix, the forum builds on South Korea’s recent $1 billion quantum investment plan and mirrors similar regional clusters in Europe and the United States that are accelerating hardware roadmaps. The networking day could translate into joint R&D programs, shared fab facilities, and a talent pipeline that shortens the time‑to‑market for quantum processors, though success will depend on aligning standards and supply‑chain logistics across both fields.
