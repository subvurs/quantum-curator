Andhra University Plans Centres of Excellence for Quantum, AI and Semiconductors

Source: The Quantum Insider September 03, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Andhra University’s plan to create dedicated Centres of Excellence for quantum technology, AI and semiconductors signals a coordinated academic push that dovetails with India’s National Quantum Mission and the global race to build home‑grown quantum ecosystems. By clustering these three strategic domains under one umbrella, the university can foster interdisciplinary research, accelerate talent development and attract industry partnerships that are essential for moving from laboratory prototypes to commercial hardware and software solutions. The initiative will be most impactful if sustained funding and clear pathways to commercialization are secured, turning the campus into a regional hub for next‑generation quantum‑enabled technologies.

— Mark Eatherly
