IBM Deploys Quantum System Two at Hyde Park Labs to Anchor Illinois Quantum Ecosystem

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a milestone for the U.S. quantum ecosystem by placing IBM’s 120‑qubit Nighthawk processor on‑premises at Chicago’s Hyde Park Labs, giving researchers direct access to one of the most powerful gate‑based machines outside of IBM’s cloud. By anchoring the National Quantum Algorithm Center and linking to initiatives in drug discovery, power‑grid optimization, and workforce development through FutureNow Chicago, the deployment accelerates both algorithmic research and talent pipelines in a region that is rapidly becoming a quantum hub. While the hardware still faces error‑rate challenges typical of near‑term devices, its local availability promises faster iteration cycles and concrete industry collaborations that could translate breakthroughs into real‑world impact sooner than cloud‑only access alone.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

IBM, the University of Chicago, and the State of Illinois have deployed an on-premises IBM Quantum System Two in Chicago, powered by the 120-qubit Nighthawk processor. This system, initially at Hyde Park Labs, will anchor the National Quantum Algorithm Center (NQAC) and support research in various fields, including drug discovery and power-grid optimization. It will also contribute to workforce development through the FutureNow Chicago center, aiming to create 750 tech jobs.
