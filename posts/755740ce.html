IonQ and EPB Partner to Launch the Tennessee Quantum Communications Research Center

Source: The Quantum Insider August 04, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first time a dedicated quantum‑communication R&D lab will be plugged directly into an operational fiber network, giving researchers immediate access to real‑world traffic for testing protocols such as QKD and entanglement distribution. By pairing IonQ’s expertise in trapped‑ion processors with EPB’s municipal infrastructure, the Tennessee Quantum Communications Research Center bridges the gap between laboratory demonstrations and scalable quantum‑network services—a step that follows recent field trials by IBM, Google and several telecoms. If successful, the center could accelerate commercial rollout of secure quantum links for finance, defense, and critical‑infrastructure communications, though scaling error‑corrected photonic channels across a city‑wide grid will still pose significant engineering challenges.

— Mark Eatherly

Insider Brief Press release – IonQ, Inc.
