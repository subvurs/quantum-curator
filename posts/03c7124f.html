Quantinuum, RIKEN, and SoftBank Partner to Advance Quantum-AI Supercomputing and Domestic Fault-Tolerant Systems

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 10, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a rare convergence of world‑class quantum hardware (Quantinuum’s Reimei H2), high‑performance GPU acceleration (RIKEN’s ROQUO) and the nation’s flagship supercomputer Fugaku, signaling Japan’s first coordinated push toward hybrid quantum‑AI supercomputing. By targeting a “Made with Japan” fault‑tolerant processor and linking it to SoftBank’s commercial AI data centers, the partnership could accelerate real‑world AI workloads that benefit from quantum speed‑ups while keeping the technology domestically controlled. The effort dovetails with global moves toward error‑corrected qubits, but readers should note that scalable fault tolerance remains a technical hurdle still under active development.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantinuum, RIKEN, and SoftBank have partnered to advance quantum-AI supercomputing and develop fault-tolerant systems in Japan. This collaboration involves commercializing hybrid quantum-AI supercomputing using Quantinuum's Reimei H2 quantum computer, RIKEN's ROQUO GPU system, and Supercomputer Fugaku. The partnership also aims to create a "Made with Japan" fault-tolerant quantum computer and integrate quantum hardware with commercial AI data centers through SoftBank.
