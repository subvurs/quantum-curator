Xanadu Unveils Technical Roadmap Targeting 1,000+ Logical Qubits by 2031

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 01, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Xanadu’s new roadmap publicly commits to delivering more than 1,000 logical qubits on a photonic platform by 2031, marking one of the most ambitious long‑term targets in the hardware race and signaling that error‑corrected photonics is moving from research toward commercial viability. By laying out concrete milestones for chip fabrication, quantum error correction thresholds and application‑driven commercialization, Xanadu aligns its strategy with industry benchmarks set by superconducting leaders such as IBM while differentiating itself through room‑temperature operation and scalability of optical interconnects. If the timeline holds, developers could begin testing algorithms that truly require fault‑tolerant resources within the next decade, though achieving the required error rates and integration density remains a formidable engineering challenge.

— Mark Eatherly
