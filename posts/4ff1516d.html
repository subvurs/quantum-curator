Bank of England Urges Firms to Move Faster on Quantum Security

Source: The Quantum Insider November 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because the Bank of England’s call for accelerated adoption of quantum‑resistant cryptography marks one of the first high‑profile regulatory pushes that treats post‑quantum security as a systemic risk rather than an optional upgrade. It dovetails with recent NIST standardization progress and the growing industry consensus that legacy RSA/ECC schemes will become vulnerable once large‑scale quantum computers emerge, highlighting the urgency for financial institutions to diversify their crypto vendors and avoid single‑point failures. For readers, the warning underscores that practical quantum‑security transitions are already entering the compliance agenda, meaning firms must budget, test, and integrate new algorithms now rather than waiting for a distant “quantum deadline.”

— Mark Eatherly
