Alice & Bob Estimates 22,593 Qubits to Break RSA-2048

Source: The Quantum Insider October 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article demonstrates that Alice & Bob’s cat‑qubit platform can theoretically factor RSA‑2048 with roughly 22,600 logical qubits—about forty times fewer physical qubits than the previous benchmark set by Google’s Craig Gidney for 2025. By slashing the required quantum resources, the result narrows the gap between today’s experimental machines and a realistic cryptographic break, underscoring how advances in error‑corrected hardware can accelerate algorithmic milestones. The claim also reminds readers that practical attacks still depend on scaling up fault‑tolerant architectures and integrating full error‑correction stacks before such capabilities become operational.

— Mark Eatherly
