Bridgewater State University Awarded $380,000 MassTech Grant to Expand Undergraduate Quantum Labs

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it signals a growing commitment from state‑level partners to build a practical quantum workforce by giving undergraduates direct access to commercial‑grade hardware in computing, sensing and cybersecurity. By equipping Bridgewater State University with three turnkey systems, the grant helps close the training gap that has limited industry pipelines despite larger national initiatives such as the IBM Q Network and NSF Quantum Leap programs. While $380 k is modest compared with federal research budgets, it provides a scalable model for other colleges to translate theoretical curricula into hands‑on experience that employers increasingly demand.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Bridgewater State University (BSU) has received approximately $380,000 from the Massachusetts Technology Collaborative (MassTech) to advance undergraduate quantum technology education, hands-on laboratory training, and workforce development. Awarded through the MassTech Technology and Innovation Ecosystem program, the grant funds the procurement of three commercial-grade quantum systems across computing, sensing, and cybersecurity.
