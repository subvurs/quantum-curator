BlueQubit Launches $150,000 “Quantum Flywheel” Compute Grant Program Supported by AWS, IBM, and NVIDIA

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 15, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights a rare convergence of industry heavyweights—IBM, AWS and NVIDIA—behind BlueQubit’s “Quantum Flywheel” grant, which hands researchers $150 000 in compute credits across QPU, GPU and CPU platforms. By bundling quantum hardware access with AI‑driven development tools, the program accelerates both algorithm discovery and error‑correction code generation at a time when scalable fault‑tolerant methods are the bottleneck for practical quantum advantage. The open‑source, peer‑reviewed focus also promises to seed reusable software stacks that could lower entry barriers for academic and startup teams alike. However, the three‑month window means only short‑term projects will benefit, so sustained impact will depend on follow‑on funding or integration into longer research pipelines.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

BlueQubit has launched the "Quantum Flywheel" grant program, offering $150,000 in compute credits over three months for quantum algorithm discovery, adversarial classical simulation, and quantum error correction research. Supported by IBM, AWS, and NVIDIA, the program provides access to QPU, GPU, and CPU hardware, along with BlueQubit's development environment, for selected research teams. The initiative aims to advance quantum computing through open-source contributions, peer-reviewed research, and AI-driven QEC code discovery.
