Haiqu Appoints Kortny Rolston-Duce to Lead Ecosystem Development and Marketing

Source: The Quantum Insider November 12, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This appointment signals Haiqu’s move from pure technology development toward building a full‑stack quantum ecosystem, a step that many emerging vendors are taking to broaden adoption beyond niche research labs. By hiring an experienced leader for ecosystem and marketing, Haiqu aims to accelerate the rollout of its quantum‑AI software across academia, government facilities and industry partners, echoing recent pushes by IBM, Microsoft and Amazon to create developer‑friendly platforms. If successful, the effort could lower barriers for scientists to experiment with quantum algorithms and help standardize tooling in a fragmented market, though the company still faces stiff competition from more established cloud providers. Readers should watch how Haiqu’s outreach translates into real‑world pilot projects, as that will be a key indicator of its impact on the growing quantum software landscape.
