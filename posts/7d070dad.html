Open Quantum Launches Native Qrisp Integration for High-Level QPU Execution Across IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, and AQT Hardware

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights a major step toward truly hardware‑agnostic quantum software by letting Qrisp’s high‑level Python abstractions compile straight to the native gates of IonQ, Rigetti, IQM and AQT devices. By removing the need for vendor‑specific low‑level code, developers can focus on algorithm design while Open Quantum handles qubit allocation and memory management, accelerating prototyping across a diverse hardware ecosystem. The move builds on recent trends such as Amazon Braket’s multi‑backend support and IBM’s Qiskit Runtime, signalling that the industry is converging on unified execution layers. However, performance will still depend on each platform’s gate fidelity and connectivity, so benchmarking native Qrisp runs remains essential.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Open Quantum has launched a native Qrisp integration, enabling high-level QPU execution across IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, and AQT hardware. This allows developers to compile and execute Qrisp programs directly on physical QPUs without writing vendor-specific gate-level code. The integration simplifies quantum programming by providing software-engineering abstractions, automated qubit allocation, and memory management.
