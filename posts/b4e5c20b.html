IBM and Ecosystem Partners Demonstrate “Trusted Quantum Advantage” Beyond Classical Supercomputers

Source: Quantum Computing Report July 30, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a milestone by showing IBM’s cloud‑based quantum processors tackling utility‑scale circuits that no current classical supercomputer can simulate, and it does so with openly published benchmarks that let the community verify the results. By teaming up with universities and startups, the effort builds on earlier “quantum advantage” demonstrations such as Google’s Sycamore but pushes the claim toward real‑world algorithms rather than synthetic tasks. The public circuit repository also creates a reusable reference point for future hardware and software improvements, even though scaling to larger problem sizes will still require continued advances in error mitigation and qubit fidelity.

— Mark Eatherly

IBM and a coalition of research collaborators—including the University of Chicago, Qedma Quantum Computing, Algorithmiq, RIKEN, and BlueQubit—have announced three joint technical demonstrations of quantum advantage.
