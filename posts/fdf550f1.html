Extension of coherence time beyond breakeven point using only drives and dissipation

Source: Nature Physics November 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article demonstrates the first autonomous error‑correction scheme that pushes a bosonic logical qubit’s coherence past the “break‑even” point using only engineered drives and dissipation, showing that hardware‑level protection can outperform even the best raw physical qubits in the same device. By eliminating the need for real‑time measurement and feedback, the protocol builds on recent cat‑code and GKP experiments and points toward more scalable, low‑overhead quantum processors. The result is a concrete proof‑of‑principle that tailored dissipation can be harnessed as a practical resource, though extending the approach to multi‑qubit operations and higher error rates will still require further engineering.

— Mark Eatherly
