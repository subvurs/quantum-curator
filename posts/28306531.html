NEC and IBM Japan Demonstrate Integrated QKD and PQC Communications

Source: The Quantum Insider November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first Japanese demonstration that a quantum‑key‑distribution link can be seamlessly combined with post‑quantum cryptographic algorithms in a single, proxy‑based validation platform, showing how legacy networks could transition to quantum‑safe security without a wholesale hardware overhaul. By pairing NEC’s mature QKD hardware with IBM Japan’s custom PQC stack, the experiment provides a concrete blueprint for hybrid defenses that protect against both present‑day attacks and future large‑scale quantum threats. It also underscores a growing industry trend toward layered cryptography, where quantum and classical safeguards are deployed together to hedge implementation risk as standards for PQC continue to evolve. The result is a practical step toward deploying quantum‑resistant communications in real‑world telecom and data‑center environments.
