Four Quantum Companies Sign-on to New Mexico’s Open-Access Quantum Network

Source: The Quantum Insider July 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first time multiple commercial players have converged on a publicly accessible entanglement‑based network, giving them a shared testbed for quantum‑secure protocols that were previously confined to isolated labs. By leveraging New Mexico’s ABQ‑Net, the companies can accelerate validation of next‑generation security technologies and demonstrate interoperability—an essential step as governments and enterprises race toward quantum‑resistant communications. The move also signals growing confidence in regional quantum infrastructure, though broader impact will depend on scaling the network beyond a single state and integrating it with national initiatives such as the DOE’s Quantum Internet roadmap.

— Mark Eatherly
