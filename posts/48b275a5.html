QED-C Roadmap Outlines Technology Requirements for Future Quantum Networks

Source: The Quantum Insider July 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because the QED‑C roadmap is the first coordinated industry effort to spell out the hardware and software milestones needed for scalable quantum networks, moving the field beyond isolated labs toward interoperable infrastructure. By linking secure communications, networked quantum computing, and distributed sensing, it ties together three fast‑growing application areas that have previously been pursued on separate tracks. The roadmap’s concrete performance targets give chip makers, photonic engineers, and standards bodies a shared reference point, though achieving the required loss‑tolerant links and error‑corrected repeaters remains a formidable technical challenge.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Insider Brief Press release – The Quantum Economic Development Consortium (QED-C) today released a new Quantum Networking Applications Roadmap, identifying the technologies and performance improvements needed to enable quantum networks for commercially valuable applications in secure communications, networked computing, and distributed sensing.
