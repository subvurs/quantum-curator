Quantum Corridor, Ciena and Toshiba Test 1.6 Tb/s Quantum-Safe Network Encryption

Source: The Quantum Insider August 04, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it shows the first real‑world demonstration of multi‑terabit per second encryption that is already resistant to future quantum attacks, proving that post‑quantum security can be layered onto existing carrier‑grade optics without a complete infrastructure overhaul. By leveraging Ciena’s WaveLogic 6 Extreme platform, the trial bridges the gap between laboratory‑scale cryptographic research and the high‑throughput demands of today’s data‑intensive services, echoing recent industry pushes such as Google’s hybrid TLS rollout and NIST’s PQC standardization efforts. While the successful Midwest test validates the technology’s speed and practicality, broader deployment will still need to address interoperability across diverse networks and the economics of scaling quantum‑safe solutions worldwide.
