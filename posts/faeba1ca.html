Diraq to Deploy Eight-Qubit Silicon Quantum Computer at Equinix Sydney Data Center

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 01, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first time a silicon‑spin qubit processor will be installed inside a commercial data center, showing that quantum hardware can move beyond isolated labs into existing cloud infrastructure. By partnering with Equinix, Diraq is demonstrating how CMOS‑compatible spin qubits could be scaled alongside conventional servers, potentially lowering costs and easing integration for hybrid quantum‑classical workloads. The eight‑qubit system slated for October 2026 also provides a tangible benchmark against which other silicon‑based efforts from Intel and academic groups can be measured. Readers should watch this deployment as an early indicator of how industry‑grade quantum services might become a routine part of data‑center offerings.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Silicon spin-qubit hardware vendor Diraq and global digital infrastructure provider Equinix, Inc. (Nasdaq: EQIX) have announced plans to deploy an eight-qubit quantum processing system inside an operational Equinix commercial data center in Sydney, Australia.
