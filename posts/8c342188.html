Q-CTRL Raises $290 Million in Series C Funding Led by Salesforce Ventures to Scale Quantum Infrastructure Software

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 13, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

AI Commentary

This article marks a watershed moment for the quantum‑software ecosystem, showing that investors now see infrastructure tools—not just hardware—as critical to unlocking scalable quantum advantage. Q‑CTRL’s $290 million Series C, led by Salesforce Ventures, underscores growing confidence that AI‑driven error mitigation and automated calibration can bridge today’s noisy intermediate‑scale devices to real‑world applications across aerospace, finance and logistics. Summary

Q-CTRL, a quantum infrastructure software provider, has raised $290 million in Series C funding led by Salesforce Ventures, valuing the company at over $1 billion. This funding will be used to expand its AI-driven control software and scale global commercial deployments in various sectors. The company's software products, Fire Opal, Boulder Opal, and Ironstone Opal, focus on quantum computer noise suppression, calibration automation, and GPS-denied quantum magnetic navigation.
