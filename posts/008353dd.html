This article matters because it signals the imminent closing of a high‑profile SPAC merger that could give Pasqal, one of Europe’s leading neutral‑atom quantum computing firms, a sizable public‑market war chest to accelerate its roadmap toward larger qubit arrays and commercial cloud services.
