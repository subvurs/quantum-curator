Pasqal Completes Business Combination with Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. II, Begins Trading on Nasdaq

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Pasqal’s SPAC merger puts the first European‑based neutral‑atom quantum computer on a major U.S. exchange, giving it a much larger pool of public capital to accelerate hardware scaling and software ecosystem development. By joining Nasdaq under the ticker PSQL, Pasqal can compete more directly with other emerging platforms such as QuEra and trapped‑ion startups that have recently secured sizable funding rounds. The move signals growing investor confidence in neutral‑atom architectures, which promise high connectivity and fast gate times for near‑term quantum advantage applications. However, as a newly public company Pasqal will now face market pressure to demonstrate rapid progress beyond its current prototype stage.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Neutral-atom quantum computing provider Pasqal has completed its previously announced business combination with special purpose acquisition company Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. II (NASDAQ: BBCQ). The combined entity now operates as Pasqal Holding SA.
