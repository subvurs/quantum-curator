Quantum Keys Travel 18 Kilometers Across Hybrid Air-and-Fiber Network

Source: The Quantum Insider August 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article demonstrates a practical step toward nationwide quantum‑secure communications by showing that free‑space QKD can be seamlessly bridged into existing fiber backbones using adaptive optics and high‑efficiency superconducting detectors. The 18‑kilometre air link, delivering roughly 1 kbps of secret key material, rivals earlier ground‑only experiments while offering the flexibility needed for metropolitan or satellite‑to‑ground networks. By proving that atmospheric turbulence can be tamed and that hybrid links scale to real‑world telecom infrastructure, the work moves quantum cryptography closer to commercial deployment, though further improvements in key rate and weather robustness will still be required.

— Mark Eatherly
