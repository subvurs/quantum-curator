Qatar Ministry of Communications and Information Technology Signs Post-Quantum Readiness Agreement with SignQuantum and QANplatform

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks one of the first high‑profile government commitments in the Middle East to embed post‑quantum cryptography into public‑sector workflows, signaling that national digital strategies are now accounting for the looming threat of quantum attacks. By pairing SignQuantum’s PQC signing suite with QANplatform’s quantum‑resistant blockchain, Qatar is testing a hybrid approach that could become a template for other ministries seeking auditable, long‑term data integrity. The move dovetails with recent standards work at NIST and growing industry pilots, but its impact will hinge on successful pilot deployments and the ability to integrate legacy systems without sacrificing performance. Readers should watch how this early adoption influences policy frameworks and commercial uptake of quantum‑safe security solutions worldwide.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology of Qatar has partnered with SignQuantum and QANplatform to explore post-quantum cryptography for public-sector data, aligning with Qatar's National Vision 2030. This collaboration, announced at MWC26 Doha, aims to enhance long-term data integrity by using SignQuantum's PQC document signing and QANplatform's quantum-resistant blockchain for secure, auditable digital records. The agreement focuses on knowledge sharing and workflow assessment, with potential future pilot deployments.
