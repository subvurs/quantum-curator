Terra Quantum Publishes White Paper Detailing Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration Paths for Blockchain Ecosystems

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 15, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it translates the abstract threat of Shor’s algorithm into concrete migration road‑maps for the blockchain platforms that underpin today’s decentralized finance ecosystem. By spotlighting Falcon‑512 as a compact, high‑throughput signature scheme, Terra Quantum bridges recent PQC standardization efforts with the practical constraints of ledger scalability—a gap that has been largely unaddressed in prior cryptography research. Summary

A white paper by Terra Quantum AG analyzes the vulnerability of major blockchain platforms like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) threats, highlighting the risks posed by Shor's algorithm to digital signature algorithms. The study evaluates PQC migration strategies, emphasizing signature payload size as a critical factor for high-throughput ledgers, and suggests FN-DSA (Falcon-512) as a viable solution due to its compact signature footprint. The paper also reviews mitigation frameworks, including Solana's WOTS vaults, Algorand's Falcon account roadmaps, and Ethereum's EIP-8052 proposal.
