Mitsubishi Electric Launches NEDO-Supported R&D to Scale Quantum Computers

Source: The Quantum Insider September 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article is noteworthy because it marks one of the first large‑scale corporate R&D programs in Japan that couples a major industrial player with government backing to push quantum hardware beyond laboratory prototypes. By securing NEDO support, Mitsubishi Electric can leverage its expertise in high‑frequency electronics and manufacturing to address scaling challenges such as cryogenic control and integration—issues that have slowed progress across the industry despite recent advances from cloud providers and startups. The partnership signals a broader policy trend toward national quantum roadmaps, suggesting that future supply chains for quantum processors may become more diversified beyond the current US‑China duopoly. Readers should keep an eye on how Mitsubishi’s roadmap aligns with global efforts to commercialize error‑corrected qubits, as any breakthroughs could accelerate practical applications in communications and materials science.

— Mark Eatherly
