NSF Renews Illinois-Led HQAN With $37.5M for Modular Quantum Computing

Source: The Quantum Insider August 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This renewed $37.5 million investment underscores the growing consensus that modular, hybrid quantum systems are the most viable path toward scalable fault‑tolerant processors, complementing the monolithic approaches pursued by industry giants. By extending NSF’s Quantum Leap Challenge Institute for Hybrid Quantum Architectures and Networks, the program will deepen collaborations between academia, national labs, and startups, accelerating hardware‑software co‑design and standards for quantum interconnects that have been missing from earlier roadmaps. The funding also signals sustained federal commitment at a time when competing nations are ramping up their own modular‑quantum initiatives, making HQAN a critical hub for maintaining U.S. leadership in the next generation of quantum hardware.

— Mark Eatherly
