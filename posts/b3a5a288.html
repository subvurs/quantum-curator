NordForsk Joins G7 Initiative to Fund International Quantum Technology Research Projects

Source: The Quantum Insider July 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because NordForsk’s entry into the G7‑coordinated quantum programme adds a substantial new source of multinational funding that will accelerate collaborative work across computing, communications, sensing and post‑quantum cryptography—areas where Europe is racing to keep pace with U.S. and Asian investments. By earmarking support for up to twelve international projects, the initiative builds on recent national roadmaps and the growing push to translate laboratory breakthroughs into industry‑ready technologies, especially in secure communications and materials science. The move also highlights a competitive landscape: success will depend on proposals that can demonstrate clear pathways from research to scalable applications, making it a key opportunity for Nordic teams to shape the next wave of quantum innovation.

— Mark Eatherly
