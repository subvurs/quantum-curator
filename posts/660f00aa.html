Source: The Quantum Insider November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first dedicated quantum‑cloud collaborations targeting South Asia, a region that has lagged behind North America and Europe in accessing on‑demand quantum resources. By pairing Quantum X’s EntangleX platform with Everest Cloud’s local infrastructure, the deal could accelerate AI‑enhanced quantum workloads for regional enterprises, research labs, and fintech firms seeking low‑latency access without building their own hardware. It also signals a broader industry trend of cloud providers forging niche partnerships to broaden the quantum ecosystem beyond traditional tech hubs, though the commercial impact will depend on how quickly EntangleX can deliver reliable performance on heterogeneous back‑ends.
