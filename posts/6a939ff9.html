Summary

The 2026 Quantum Open Source Survey, with over 1,800 respondents, reveals key trends in quantum software adoption, hybrid workflows, and AI tooling. Qiskit leads in SDKs, IBM Quantum in cloud services, and Anthropic Claude Code in AI assistants. The survey also highlights the growing use of Python and C/C++, challenges in QEC and HPC, and cautious optimism regarding AI's role in quantum development.
