Quantum Alliance Launches to Set Post-Quantum Standards for Blockchains

Source: The Quantum Insider October 15, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first coordinated effort to codify what “quantum‑ready” really means for blockchain ecosystems, a crucial step as emerging quantum computers threaten the elliptic‑curve signatures that underlie most public ledgers. By aligning with broader post‑quantum cryptography work such as NIST’s standardization process, the Quantum Alliance aims to prevent a patchwork of ad‑hoc solutions and give developers clear criteria for evaluating and migrating to vetted algorithms. If the standards gain traction, they could smooth the transition for dozens of networks while also highlighting that practical quantum attacks are still years away, so early adoption must be balanced against performance and interoperability trade‑offs.

— Mark Eatherly
