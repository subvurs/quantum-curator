This article matters because it provides one of the first systematic benchmarks of how quickly financial firms are moving from exploratory pilots to production‑grade quantum projects, a signal that the sector is reaching critical mass. By more than doubling its coverage to 64 institutions across eight regions, the 2026 Finance Quantum Index captures the geographic diversification and growing competitive pressure that are driving banks to secure early‑stage quantum talent and hardware access.
