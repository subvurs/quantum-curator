QNu Labs Introduces Corporate Quantum-Safe Training Supported by National Quantum Mission

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it signals a maturing ecosystem where governments and vendors are teaming up to close the skills gap ahead of practical quantum attacks on today’s cryptography. By tying its corporate‑focused curriculum to India’s National Quantum Mission, QNu Labs joins other industry moves—such as NIST’s post‑quantum standardization effort and major cloud providers’ quantum‑security roadmaps—to ensure security teams can evaluate quantum communication and PQC solutions now rather than later. The rollout of intensive workshops and executive briefings gives enterprises a concrete pathway to start hardening their data pipelines, though the urgency must be balanced against the still‑uncertain timeline for large‑scale fault‑tolerant quantum computers.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

QNu Labs has launched a corporate quantum-safe training program, supported by India's National Quantum Mission, to prepare security professionals for quantum threats. The program covers quantum communication, post-quantum cryptography, and includes case studies from QNu Labs' deployments. This initiative complements their commercial strategy and is offered as intensive workshops and executive briefings.
