DARPA Awards Contract to Qunnect to Advance Real-Time Polarization Compensation for Quantum Networks

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 13, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because real‑time polarization compensation is a key missing piece for maintaining high‑fidelity entanglement over existing telecom fiber, a prerequisite for any practical quantum internet. DARPA’s investment in Qunnect under the QuANET and SBIR programs builds on recent advances in integrated photonic repeaters and error‑corrected links, pushing hardware from laboratory demos toward field‑ready networks that could support secure communications and distributed sensing. While the contract signals strong government‑industry momentum, scaling the technology to a nationwide backbone will still require further engineering and standards work.

— Mark Eatherly
