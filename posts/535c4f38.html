Ahead of EU Quantum Act, IQM Calls for Dedicated Qubit Foundries and DARPA-Style Procurement

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 03, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it spotlights a strategic gap in Europe’s quantum hardware pipeline—namely the absence of dedicated qubit foundries—and proposes a DARPA‑style procurement model that mirrors successful U.S. initiatives such as the National Quantum Initiative. By urging the EU to back late‑stage growth capital and create specialized fabs, IQM is trying to turn Europe’s strong public funding and early QPU deployments into a self‑sustaining ecosystem capable of competing with American and Asian players. The proposal is ambitious, but its impact will hinge on whether policymakers can move quickly enough to fund multi‑billion‑dollar fab projects while navigating the continent’s complex regulatory landscape.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

A new report by IQM Quantum Computers, "Quantum Is Europe's Second Chance," calls for the EU to implement a Quantum Act to compete with other global powers in quantum computing. The report highlights Europe's strengths in public investment and QPU deployments but points out weaknesses in venture capital, chip manufacturing, and regulatory speed. It proposes dedicated qubit foundries, increased late-stage growth capital, and DARPA-style procurement to foster a robust European quantum ecosystem.
