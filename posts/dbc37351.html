BlueQubit Releases AI-Engineered State-Vector Simulator Outperforming NVIDIA cuStateVec Benchmarks

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights BlueQubit’s new bluesv simulator, which leverages AI‑driven optimizations to squeeze more performance out of GPUs than NVIDIA’s own cuStateVec and Google’s qsim—benchmarks that have long set the bar for high‑fidelity state‑vector simulation. By offering drop‑in API compatibility with popular frameworks such as Qiskit and Cirq, bluesv could lower the engineering overhead for researchers and accelerate algorithm prototyping on existing hardware. The open‑source release also signals a shift toward community‑driven performance tuning, though real‑world gains will still depend on problem size, GPU architecture, and how well the AI‑generated kernels generalize beyond the test suites used in the paper.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

BlueQubit has developed bluesv, an AI-engineered quantum state-vector simulator that outperforms NVIDIA’s cuStateVec and Google's qsim in benchmark tests. It offers direct API compatibility with major quantum programming frameworks and will be released as open-source software, representing a significant advancement in quantum simulation technology.
