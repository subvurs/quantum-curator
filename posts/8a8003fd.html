E.ON and Qoro Test Hybrid Quantum Computing for Energy Scheduling

Source: The Quantum Insider October 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks one of the first real‑world demonstrations that hybrid quantum‑classical algorithms can tackle the notoriously hard optimization problem of peer‑to‑peer energy scheduling, showing how a decomposition‑based pipeline built on Qoro’s open‑source SDK can be integrated with existing grid software. It builds on recent advances in variational and annealing approaches that have been tested on small benchmark instances, moving the field from laboratory curiosities toward practical utility for utilities like E.ON. While the proof‑of‑concept is promising, scaling to larger networks will still require more qubits and error mitigation before commercial deployment becomes feasible.

— Mark Eatherly
