FPT Corporation and Niels Bohr Institute Partner to Advance Quantum Research and Talent Development in Vietnam and Denmark

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This partnership is notable because it links a leading Southeast Asian tech firm with one of Europe’s most prestigious quantum research centers, creating a pipeline for talent and joint projects that could accelerate Vietnam’s nascent quantum computing roadmap. By combining FPT’s industry resources with the Niels Bohr Institute’s scientific expertise, the collaboration mirrors a broader trend of multinational alliances aimed at closing the skills gap and fostering regional ecosystems beyond the traditional hubs in North America and Europe. If successful, the program could position both countries as strategic nodes in the global quantum supply chain while offering students and researchers hands‑on experience with cutting‑edge hardware and algorithms.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

FPT Corporation and the Niels Bohr Institute have partnered to advance quantum research and talent development in Vietnam and Denmark. The collaboration, formalized during the Vietnam–Denmark Business Forum, will focus on joint research programs, academic exchanges, and workshops. This initiative aims to accelerate Vietnam’s quantum computing roadmap and strengthen Denmark's research ties with Southeast Asia.
