Alice & Bob Reports Cat-Qubit Progress in French Quantum Computing Program

Source: The Quantum Insider October 20, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a tangible step toward scalable fault‑tolerant processors by showing that Alice & Bob’s cat‑qubit architecture can meet the performance targets set in France’s PROQCIMA programme, which is one of the few national initiatives explicitly linking quantum hardware to defense and industrial roadmaps. The reported Phase 1 results—demonstrating long coherence times and error rates approaching the break‑even threshold for bosonic codes—place European efforts on a comparable footing with parallel advances at IBM, Google and academic labs pursuing surface‑code or trapped‑ion approaches. While still prototype‑level, the work signals that coordinated government funding can accelerate the transition from laboratory demonstrations to deployable quantum modules, a development that could reshape both national security computing strategies and the broader commercial supply chain.

— Mark Eatherly
