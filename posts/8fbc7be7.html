D-Wave and LG CNS Partner on Quantum Optimization in South Korea

Source: The Quantum Insider October 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first major South Korean AI firm to team up with a dual‑platform quantum provider, giving LG CNS direct access to D‑Wave’s annealing machines and gate‑model processors for tackling large‑scale optimization problems in logistics, finance and manufacturing. The partnership reflects a broader industry shift toward hybrid quantum‑classical workflows, echoing recent collaborations by IBM, Google and other startups that aim to move quantum algorithms out of the lab and into enterprise pilots. While the deal promises faster proof‑of‑concept deployments, its impact will still depend on D‑Wave’s ability to scale qubit counts and improve error rates before truly competitive advantages emerge.

— Mark Eatherly
