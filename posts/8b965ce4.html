IBM to Acquire HRL Laboratories to Expand Quantum Roadmap with Silicon-Spin Qubits

Source: Quantum Computing Report July 23, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks IBM’s first major move to broaden its quantum‑hardware portfolio beyond superconducting circuits by bringing silicon‑spin qubit expertise in‑house through the HRL acquisition. By adding a platform that promises tighter integration with existing semiconductor manufacturing, IBM can pursue a parallel scaling path and potentially lower error rates, positioning itself against rivals such as Google’s superconducting chips and IonQ’s trapped‑ion systems. The deal also signals deeper industry collaboration—Boeing and GM remain stakeholders—though the practical impact will depend on how quickly silicon‑spin devices can meet coherence and control benchmarks that have so far favored superconductors.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

IBM (NYSE: IBM) has signed a definitive agreement to acquire HRL Laboratories, LLC (HRL), the Malibu-based research and development institution jointly owned by Boeing and General Motors. The acquisition expands IBM’s quantum hardware strategy, adding HRL’s expertise in silicon-spin qubits alongside its core superconducting qubit program.
