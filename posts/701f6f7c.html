Sparrow Quantum Sets Record With 500 Million Usable Photons Per Second

Source: The Quantum Insider September 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article is noteworthy because Sparrow’s new record of 500 million usable photons per second pushes photonic hardware into a regime where truly scalable quantum processors and long‑distance quantum networking become realistic, closing the gap with superconducting and trapped‑ion platforms that have traditionally delivered higher raw gate rates. The breakthrough builds on recent advances in low‑loss waveguides, high‑efficiency detectors and time‑bin multiplexing, showing that integrated photonics can now generate and manipulate photons fast enough to support error‑corrected logical qubits and real‑time quantum communication. While the rate is impressive, practical deployment will still depend on reducing loss across fiber links and integrating reliable photon‑source arrays into larger architectures.

— Mark Eatherly

Insider Brief PRESS RELEASE — Quantum computers are being built in several ways: superconducting circuits, trapped ions, neutral atoms, semiconductor spins and photonics. Photonic quantum systems have one fundamental advantage: their information carriers can travel through optical fibre, making the same technology relevant to both quantum processors and quantum networks.
