NIST Releases Final IR 8547 Establishing Definitive Post-Quantum Cryptography Transition Timeline and Deprecation Schedules

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because NIST’s final IR 8547 turns the long‑running PQC standardization effort into a concrete policy timetable that will force every federal system—and eventually most commercial services—to replace RSA/ECDSA with quantum‑resistant algorithms within the next decade. The deadlines align with recent breakthroughs in lattice‑based and code‑based schemes that have cleared security evaluations, making the transition technically feasible while still giving vendors time to implement hybrid deployments. By flagging “Harvest Now, Decrypt Later” threats, the roadmap underscores an urgent risk for any organization still relying on classical public‑key crypto, prompting immediate migration planning. Readers should note that while the schedule is binding for U.S. agencies, global adoption will likely follow, reshaping key‑management ecosystems worldwide.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

NIST has released its final roadmap, NIST IR 8547, for transitioning to post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards, with federal agencies facing binding deadlines to adopt PQC for key establishment by December 31, 2030, and digital signatures by December 31, 2031. Classical public-key algorithms will be deprecated after 2030 and disallowed after 2035. The report emphasizes the urgency of PQC adoption due to "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" attacks and provides guidelines for hybrid schemes during the transition.
