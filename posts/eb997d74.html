IonQ | IonQ and SDT Announce the First Strategic Partnership to Bring Both Advanced Quantum Computing and Quantum Networking to the Asia-Pacific Region

Source: IonQ Blog September 25, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a milestone in the global rollout of trapped‑ion technology, as IonQ’s Superion 256 processor and its first silicon‑vacancy quantum memory module will be installed in South Korea through a strategic partnership with SDT. By coupling a high‑fidelity ion‑trap computer with a dedicated quantum‑networking node, the deployment gives Asia‑Pacific researchers and enterprises direct access to both compute and storage capabilities that have so far been confined to a handful of US labs. While the hardware promises unprecedented coherence times and error rates for near‑term applications, its practical impact will depend on building the surrounding software stack and ecosystem to exploit the new quantum networking link.

— Mark Eatherly
