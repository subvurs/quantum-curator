Source: The Quantum Insider November 11, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a major milestone in IBM’s roadmap, as the Nighthawk r3 processor aims to double the qubit count while delivering 10,000‑gate fidelity on half of those qubits—a combination that pushes quantum volume into territory needed for practical chemistry and optimization workloads. By targeting a 1,040‑qubit system by 2027, IBM is positioning itself ahead of rivals who are still grappling with scaling beyond a few hundred noisy qubits, and the announced interconnect strategy hints at early steps toward modular, error‑corrected architectures. However, achieving consistent gate performance across such a large array remains a formidable engineering challenge, so the true impact will depend on how quickly IBM can translate these specifications into reliable, reproducible runs for end users.

— Mark Eatherly
