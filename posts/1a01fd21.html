Diraq Opens Santa Monica Hub to Expand Silicon Quantum Computing R&D

Source: The Quantum Insider August 26, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Diraq’s new Santa Monica hub consolidates a dedicated silicon‑spin‑qubit team in one of the world’s densest pools of quantum talent, accelerating the push to bring qubits onto a CMOS‑compatible platform. By situating its R&D alongside other Southern California initiatives—from Intel’s silicon‑based processors to academic spin‑qubit breakthroughs—Diraq can more rapidly iterate on fabrication techniques that promise higher integration density and lower cost than superconducting approaches. The move underscores the growing belief that scalable, industry‑grade quantum hardware will likely emerge from silicon, though achieving fault‑tolerant error rates remains a formidable hurdle.

— Mark Eatherly
