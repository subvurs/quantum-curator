Source: The Quantum Insider July 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights a breakthrough architecture that could finally overcome the interconnect bottleneck limiting today’s quantum chips, enabling coherent communication among millions of qubits spread across a single wafer. By demonstrating long‑range coupling without excessive wiring or thermal load, the Warwick–NRC design dovetails with recent modular and photonic‑link approaches that aim to stitch together smaller processors into larger fault‑tolerant systems. If the concept scales as promised, it would bring the long‑term goal of a million‑qubit machine closer to reality, though experimental validation at larger qubit counts will be essential before industry adoption.
