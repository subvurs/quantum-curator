Crypto4A QASM Module Achieves FIPS 140-3 Level 3 Validation

Source: The Quantum Insider August 21, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Crypto4A’s QASM™ module is the first cryptographic product to earn FIPS 140‑3 Level 3 validation, giving governments and critical‑infrastructure operators a formally vetted quantum‑resistant solution today rather than waiting for future standards. The certification dovetails with the ongoing NIST post‑quantum cryptography standardization effort, showing that practical, high‑assurance implementations can keep pace with emerging threats from quantum computers. While the validation is a major milestone, broader adoption will still depend on ecosystem support and the ability to update algorithms as the PQC landscape evolves.

— Mark Eatherly
