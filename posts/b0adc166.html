QFactorial and Monash University Secure A$1.12 Million ($$758.6K USD) Australian Government Grant for Quantum Logistics Optimization

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 14, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

By targeting freight and last‑mile delivery—domains where classical combinatorial solvers already strain under massive data volumes—the project could demonstrate tangible speedups or cost reductions that validate quantum advantage beyond academic benchmarks. Summary

QFactorial and Monash University have received a A$1.12 million Australian government grant to develop a hybrid quantum-classical optimization solver for freight and last-mile delivery. The 15-month project aims to advance QFactorial's routing and fleet-utilization platform. This initiative, part of the Critical Technologies Challenge Program, involves a consortium including IBM Quantum, AWS, and Fujitsu, and seeks to overcome limitations of classical solvers in large-scale logistics.
