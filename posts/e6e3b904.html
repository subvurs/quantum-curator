UK NQCC Partners With Phasecraft to Expand Access to Quantum Software

Source: The Quantum Insider November 06, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This partnership is significant because it gives a broad swath of UK businesses— from research labs to midsize firms—direct access to Phasecraft’s ready‑to‑run quantum algorithms, turning the NQCC’s national infrastructure into an actionable software platform rather than just hardware. By bundling an enterprise licence with the centre’s cloud resources, the deal mirrors similar ecosystem moves in the US and Europe that aim to lower the barrier between algorithm development and real‑world problem solving, accelerating pilot projects in finance, materials and logistics. The collaboration also signals strong policy backing for a coordinated quantum supply chain, though its impact will ultimately depend on how quickly hardware providers can deliver qubits with sufficient fidelity to run those algorithms at scale.

— Mark Eatherly
