Guest Post: Why Crypto-Agility Matters for Financial Institutions Preparing for PQC

Source: The Quantum Insider November 04, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article spotlights a pressing gap in the financial sector’s security roadmap: most banks still run on legacy RSA and ECC schemes that a cryptographically relevant quantum computer could break. By urging “crypto‑agility,” it aligns with the recent NIST PQC standardization effort and gives institutions a concrete path to swap algorithms without overhauling entire infrastructures. The piece is timely because regulators are now asking for migration plans, and early adopters can avoid costly retrofits while protecting billions in transactions. It also cautions that agility alone won’t suffice unless firms pair it with rigorous risk assessments and phased rollouts of vetted post‑quantum primitives.

— Mark Eatherly
