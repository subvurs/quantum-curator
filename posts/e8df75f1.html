Source: The Quantum Insider November 23, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article is noteworthy because it marks Microsoft’s first dedicated effort to commercialize post‑quantum readiness as a service, moving the conversation from academic research to actionable tooling for enterprises. By pairing AI‑driven discovery with expertise in quantum‑vulnerable cryptography, the Israeli team aims to surface legacy algorithms before large‑scale quantum computers become a realistic threat—a concern that has intensified after recent advances in error‑corrected qubit counts. The initiative dovetails with industry‑wide moves such as NIST’s standardization process and other vendors’ migration roadmaps, giving organizations a concrete pathway rather than vague compliance checklists. However, the effectiveness of AI flagging will still depend on accurate inventory data and timely updates to emerging post‑quantum standards.
