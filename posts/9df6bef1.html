FirstQFM Demonstrates Quantum Reservoir Computing on Rigetti’s 108-Qubit Processor to Outperform Classical Financial Benchmarks

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 03, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows the first practical use‑case where a noisy intermediate‑scale quantum (NISQ) processor can beat state‑of‑the‑art classical models on a real‑world financial forecasting task, highlighting quantum reservoir computing as a latency‑friendly way to harness large qubit arrays. By leveraging Rigetti’s 108‑qubit chip, FirstQFM demonstrates that even without full fault tolerance, quantum‑enhanced time‑series analysis can deliver higher Sharpe ratios and profit metrics than leading machine‑learning baselines. The result builds on recent work exploring hybrid quantum‑classical learning architectures and suggests a near‑term pathway for quant firms to experiment with NISQ hardware, though the advantage remains task‑specific and does not yet constitute a universal quantum speedup.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

FirstQFM, in collaboration with SW7 Group and Rigetti Computing, has successfully demonstrated a financial forecasting pilot using quantum reservoir computing (QRC) on Rigetti’s 108-qubit processor. This QRC system outperformed classical machine learning models and time-series foundation models in financial benchmarks, achieving significantly higher cumulative gross profit and loss and Sharpe ratios. The study highlights QRC as a promising and latency-efficient approach for deploying NISQ processors in quantitative finance, despite not claiming general quantum advantage.
