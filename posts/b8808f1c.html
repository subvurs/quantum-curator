Qarakal Quantum And Quantum Elements Receive BIRD Foundation Grant For Quantum Computing Innovation With a $2.4 Million Budget

Source: The Quantum Insider October 28, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article is noteworthy because it funds a cross‑border effort to build a quantum‑simulation platform that can model surface chemistry—a problem that still strains classical supercomputers and has been a key target for early fault‑tolerant quantum advantage. By focusing on catalytic reactions, the Qarakal Quantum–Quantum Elements collaboration aligns with recent pushes from IBM, Google and academic consortia to demonstrate practical quantum chemistry use cases beyond gas‑phase molecules. If successful, the platform could accelerate materials discovery for energy conversion and industrial processes, though the $2.4 million budget underscores that scaling to chemically relevant system sizes will still require significant hardware advances.

— Mark Eatherly
