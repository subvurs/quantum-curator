Pasqal and KACST Partner to Advance Post-Quantum Cryptography in Saudi Arabia

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 01, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first high‑profile collaborations between a neutral‑atom quantum processor vendor and a national research agency to tackle post‑quantum cryptography (PQC) readiness, signaling that Saudi Arabia is moving beyond theoretical studies toward hardware‑enabled security testing. By pairing Pasqal’s analog neutral‑atom platform with KACST’s National Center for Quantum Technologies, the partnership could provide a rare testbed for evaluating PQC algorithms on real quantum hardware, accelerating both standards development and local talent pipelines. The initiative also dovetails with worldwide efforts to harden critical infrastructure ahead of large‑scale fault‑tolerant machines, though the practical impact will depend on how quickly Pasqal’s devices mature beyond laboratory prototypes.

— Mark Eatherly
