Texas Quantum Partners and Samara Unveil Site Master Plan for Occam Foundry Tech Campus in Austin

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 18, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first large‑scale, purpose‑built quantum campus in Austin, a city already emerging as a hotbed for semiconductor and AI talent. By clustering university spinouts, startups, and hardware operations on a single 16.5‑acre site, Occam Foundry aims to accelerate collaboration and reduce the “valley of death” that often stalls quantum ventures—a strategy echoed by recent hub initiatives in Europe and at IBM’s Q Network. If the campus can attract a critical mass of projects, it could cement Texas as a new node in the global quantum ecosystem, though its impact will ultimately hinge on securing enough tenant pipelines and sustained investment.

— Mark Eatherly
