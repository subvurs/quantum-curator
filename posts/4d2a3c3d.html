Florida Atlantic University Launches Quantum Computing Business Strategy Certificate

Source: The Quantum Insider August 06, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights how higher‑education institutions are moving to fill the emerging talent gap by giving business leaders a structured way to grasp quantum computing’s strategic implications, something that has become increasingly urgent as firms like IBM, Microsoft and AWS roll out enterprise‑focused quantum services. By offering an eight‑week certificate, FAU is joining a growing wave of executive‑education programs that aim to translate technical breakthroughs—such as error‑corrected qubits and cloud‑based quantum access—into concrete sourcing and partnership decisions. While the curriculum can’t replace deep technical expertise, it equips decision‑makers with the vocabulary and risk‑assessment tools needed to position their companies for a technology that is still in its early commercial phase.
