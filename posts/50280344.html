CO.LAB Selects Four Startups for Inaugural CO.LAB Q Commercialization Studio in Chattanooga

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a concrete step toward turning quantum research into market-ready products by establishing the first CO.LAB Q commercialization studio in Chattanooga, where four early‑stage startups will gain access to regional testbeds such as the EPB Quantum Center and expertise from partners like Quantinuum and Oak Ridge National Laboratory. By bundling technical resources, industry mentorship, and potential customers in a focused 12‑month program, the initiative mirrors larger efforts such as IBM’s Q Network but brings quantum development to a new geographic hub, diversifying the ecosystem beyond traditional coastal clusters. The real impact will hinge on whether these startups can translate limited hardware access into viable applications—particularly in sensing, optimization or error mitigation—before scaling challenges and supply constraints catch up.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

CO.LAB has launched its first CO.LAB Q commercialization studio in Chattanooga, featuring four startups: RidgeQuantum, Superquantum, esoteriq, and Kottackal Business Solutions. This 12-month program, starting November 2026, aims to commercialize quantum technologies by connecting these companies with regional quantum testbeds, technical partners, and potential customers. The program leverages Chattanooga's quantum infrastructure, including the EPB Quantum Center, and is supported by various partners like EPB, Quantinuum, and Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
