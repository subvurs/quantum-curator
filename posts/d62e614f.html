Source: The Quantum Insider July 31, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article spotlights a often‑overlooked bottleneck in the post‑quantum transition: the need for crypto‑agile architectures that can swap in new algorithms quickly and safely, rather than simply picking the “right” scheme from NIST’s shortlist. As enterprises begin to harden TLS stacks and IoT firmware against quantum attacks, built‑in agility will determine whether they face costly, service‑disrupting overhauls or can roll out updates incrementally. The piece therefore frames crypto‑agility as a prerequisite for realistic, low‑risk migration, reminding readers that algorithm choice alone won’t secure future‑proof security without flexible system design.
