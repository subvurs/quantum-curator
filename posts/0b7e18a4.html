Two paths, one flux: Trapped ions simulate Aharonov–Bohm interference in a lattice gauge theory

Source: Phys.org Quantum November 07, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first experimental observation of Aharonov–Bohm interference within a lattice‑gauge‑theory simulation on a trapped‑ion platform, showing that hybrid ion processors can faithfully encode topological phases of gauge fields. By reproducing a hallmark high‑energy‑physics phenomenon in a controllable quantum device, the work bridges the gap between abstract lattice models and near‑term hardware, complementing recent superconducting‑circuit efforts to simulate gauge dynamics. The result not only validates trapped ions as a viable testbed for complex field theories but also paves the way toward scalable simulations of particle‑physics processes once error rates are further reduced.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

In a new Nature Physics study, researchers demonstrated the Aharonov-Bohm effect in a quantum simulation of a lattice gauge theory using a hybrid trapped-ion quantum computer.
