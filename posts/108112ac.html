Quantum Computing Inc. Signs Three-Year Framework Agreement with Hamad Bin Khalifa University for Middle East QPU Deployment

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first time a U.S. quantum‑hardware vendor is committing to install a dedicated QPU—Quantum Computing Inc.’s Dirac‑3 Entropy machine—in the Gulf region, signaling a shift from purely cloud‑based access toward on‑premises quantum resources for industry and academia. By pairing hardware deployment with joint research, workforce training, and sensing/communications projects, the partnership dovetails with recent moves by governments in the Middle East to build national quantum strategies and could accelerate real‑world optimization use cases such as logistics or energy grid management. However, the practical impact will depend on how quickly the Dirac‑3 can deliver error‑corrected performance beyond current noisy intermediate‑scale devices. Readers should watch this collaboration as a barometer for regional adoption of quantum computing infrastructure.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantum Computing Inc. has signed a three-year agreement with Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Qatar, focusing on quantum computing, sensing, and communications. This partnership will establish a Dirac-3 Entropy Quantum Computer in Qatar, offering cloud access and on-premises hardware for advanced optimization problems.
