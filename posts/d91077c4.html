AI Commentary

This article highlights how silicon—once the workhorse of classical computing—is now emerging as a leading platform for scalable quantum processors, leveraging decades‑long CMOS manufacturing expertise to accelerate qubit production. By tracing the material’s evolution from simple sand‑derived semiconductors to high‑coherence spin and superconducting qubits, it underscores recent breakthroughs such as sub‑100 ns two‑qubit gates and error rates approaching fault‑tolerance thresholds that bring silicon quantum chips closer to practical integration with existing hardware ecosystems. Readers should care because a successful silicon‑based quantum roadmap could dramatically lower costs, speed up deployment, and enable hybrid classical‑quantum systems without the need for entirely new fabrication facilities.
