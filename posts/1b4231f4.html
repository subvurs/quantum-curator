Xanadu and Mitsubishi Chemical Advance Quantum Computing Work on EUV Lithography

Source: The Quantum Insider August 25, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it showcases one of the first deep‑tech partnerships that applies photonic quantum processors to a concrete bottleneck in chip manufacturing—optimising EUV lithography patterns and process windows. By leveraging Xanadu’s latest Gaussian boson sampling and variational algorithms, the collaboration aims to accelerate material‑design cycles and reduce costly trial‑and‑error runs, complementing recent industry moves such as IBM’s roadmap for quantum‑enhanced design automation. If successful, the work could translate into tighter feature control and higher yields for next‑generation nodes, though the practical impact will still depend on scaling the hardware to fault‑tolerant levels and integrating the software stack into existing fab workflows.

— Mark Eatherly
