IBM Unveils Meter-Scale Superconducting L-Couplers: Connecting Dual 120-Qubit Processors with 99% State Transfer Fidelity

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights IBM’s breakthrough in scaling quantum interconnects by demonstrating meter‑scale superconducting L‑couplers that link two 120‑qubit chips with 99 % state‑transfer fidelity, a key step toward modular fault‑tolerant architectures. By solving the long‑standing challenge of high‑fidelity communication over macroscopic distances, the work paves the way for larger distributed processors such as the upcoming IBM Quantum Starling system and brings practical quantum networking closer to reality. The result also shows that superconducting hardware can maintain coherence across physically separated modules, a prerequisite for scaling beyond the current monolithic designs. Readers should note that while 99 % fidelity is impressive, further improvements will be needed to meet the error thresholds required for full error‑corrected computation.

Summary

IBM Quantum has achieved a significant milestone in quantum computing with the development of meter-scale superconducting L-couplers. These couplers successfully connect two 120-qubit processors with 99% quantum state transfer fidelity. This innovation addresses crucial engineering challenges for building modular fault-tolerant quantum computers, paving the way for future advancements like the IBM Quantum Starling processor.
