Anderon Releases First Superconducting Quantum PDK for 300mm Wafer Fabrication

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 15, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a pivotal step toward industrializing superconducting qubit production by giving designers a mature, silicon‑style PDK that can be used on Anderon’s 300 mm fab line—a scale previously limited to research‑grade 100–150 mm processes. By standardising layout rules and offering ready‑made I/O, amplifier and filter blocks, the kit could dramatically shorten design cycles and improve yield consistency across multiple foundries, echoing the recent push by IBM and Google toward fab‑scale qubit arrays. The move also signals that large‑volume manufacturing is becoming a realistic business model for quantum hardware, though early adopters will still need to validate performance on these new wafer formats before large‑scale deployment.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Anderon, a quantum foundry operator, has released its first Superconducting Quantum Process Design Kit (PDK) for 300mm wafer fabrication. This PDK provides standardized design rules and building blocks for manufacturing superconducting quantum devices on their 300mm wafer line at the Albany NanoTech Complex. Anderon, backed by $2 billion in funding, offers various superconducting wafer types, including qubit, I/O signal, and amplifier/filter wafers.
