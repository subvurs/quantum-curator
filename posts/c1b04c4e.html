AI Commentary

This article showcases a hands‑on university capstone that leverages AWS’s quantum‑cloud tools to prototype noise‑mitigation strategies on real superconducting hardware, highlighting how accessible cloud platforms are becoming testbeds for next‑generation error‑reduction techniques. By integrating hardware‑aware calibration routines with emerging sensing methods, the project bridges the gap between theoretical mitigation algorithms and practical, scalable workflows that could extend circuit depth in today’s NISQ devices.
