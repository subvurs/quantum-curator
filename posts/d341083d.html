Source: The Quantum Insider November 16, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights how the Qollab × IonQ hackathon turned a global, largely novice crowd into active quantum‑software developers, underscoring the rapid expansion of the quantum talent pipeline that industry and academia have been racing to build. By attracting over a thousand participants from 37 countries—including engineers with no prior quantum experience—it demonstrates that accessible cloud‑based platforms and open learning resources are lowering entry barriers faster than many earlier university‑centric programs. The surge in grassroots coding effort not only seeds a broader developer community for IonQ’s hardware but also accelerates the feedback loop needed to refine tools, benchmark algorithms, and ultimately bring practical quantum applications closer to reality.
