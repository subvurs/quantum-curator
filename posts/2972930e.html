Dissipation Can Be a Tool For Entanglement

Source: The Quantum Insider August 10, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it flips a long‑standing assumption that environmental loss is purely detrimental, showing instead that carefully engineered dissipation can be harnessed to produce high‑fidelity entanglement between qubits. The result builds on recent advances in reservoir engineering and dissipative state preparation—techniques already demonstrated in trapped ions and superconducting circuits—and suggests a new pathway for generating the entangled resources needed for quantum communication and error‑corrected computation without ultra‑precise control pulses. While promising, the approach still requires precise tailoring of the system–bath coupling, so scaling it to large processor architectures will be an important next challenge.

— Mark Eatherly
