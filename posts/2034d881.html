Quantinuum Details Nexus Hybrid Workflow Runtime Integration with Slurm and OpenQSE at SC26

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it shows Quantinuum’s Nexus platform moving beyond isolated quantum tasks to a fully integrated hybrid workflow that can be scheduled alongside traditional HPC jobs via Slurm and OpenQSE, dramatically cutting idle time on classical nodes while waiting for quantum processors. By coupling Tierkreis’s runtime with a real‑world electronic‑structure calculation—delivering lower ground‑state energies for a 12‑atom hydrogen chain—the work demonstrates how near‑term quantum sampling can already add value to scientific pipelines, echoing recent pushes from IBM and AWS toward cloud‑native quantum runtimes. The breakthrough highlights the practical path toward scalable quantum‑classical co‑execution, even as hardware noise and limited qubit counts remain constraints that future generations will need to overcome.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Quantinuum's Nexus developer platform and Hybrid Workflow Runtime (Tierkreis) integrate quantum programming with supercomputing workload managers like Slurm, as demonstrated at SC26. This integration coordinates multi-stage scientific pipelines, preventing classical node idling while quantum jobs await processing. They also showcased a joint electronic-structure workflow with RIKEN, achieving lower ground-state energies for a 12-atom hydrogen chain through quantum-sampled electronic configurations.
