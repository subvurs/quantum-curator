ColibriTD Raises €4 Million ($4.66 Million USD) Seed Round to Scale Multiphysics Quantum Simulation Software

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This seed round signals growing investor confidence that multiphysics quantum simulation—once a niche research tool—is becoming essential for designing next‑generation materials and devices across industries. ColibriTD’s expanded algorithm stack builds on recent advances in hybrid quantum‑classical workflows that promise more accurate modeling of complex phenomena than classical supercomputers alone can deliver. By scaling internationally, the startup could accelerate the transition from proof‑of‑concept simulations to practical engineering applications, though its impact will still hinge on the availability of sufficiently large and error‑corrected quantum hardware.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Paris-based quantum software startup ColibriTD has closed a €4 million ($4.66 million USD) Seed funding round led by Earlybird Venture Capital, with participation from SymbiaVC and Medin VC.
