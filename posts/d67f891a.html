NQCP Spins Out Pelagic Systems to Commercialize Real-Time Hardware Calibration Software

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 29, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Pelagic Systems tackles one of the most pressing bottlenecks in quantum hardware—continuous, low‑latency calibration of multi‑qubit devices—by moving the tuning loops onto FPGA controllers and delivering real‑time feedback. The approach complements recent pushes from IBM, Google and Rigetti to embed control software closer to the chip, promising faster error mitigation and higher uptime as processors scale beyond a few dozen qubits. If the commercial platform lives up to its claims, it could make routine hardware maintenance almost autonomous, accelerating both research cycles and the rollout of practical quantum services.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Pelagic Systems, a spin-out from the Novo Nordisk Foundation Quantum Computing Programme (NQCP), is commercializing software for real-time control, calibration, and characterization of quantum processors. Their platform addresses parameter instabilities in multi-qubit systems by running routines directly on FPGA controllers, significantly reducing latency compared to traditional methods. This technology aims to automate and streamline the tuning of complex quantum hardware, offering a solution to a growing bottleneck in the industry.
