Ancilla-Free Exact Dicke-State Synthesis with Optimal Size and Depth

Source: arXiv Quantum Physics November 20, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article delivers the first ancilla‑free construction of exact Dicke‑state circuits that reaches both optimal linear gate count and logarithmic depth for any Hamming weight, a milestone because Dicke states are key resources for quantum metrology, error‑correcting codes and variational algorithms. By disproving the previously conjectured Ω(k) depth bound for balanced Dicke states, it shows that large‑scale symmetric entanglement can be generated far more efficiently than thought, opening the door to near‑term hardware demonstrations on modest qubit arrays. The constant‑time amplitude‑amplification step also means the method scales cleanly without costly iterative subroutines, although practical performance will still depend on native two‑qubit gate fidelity and connectivity. Readers should watch how this technique is adopted in quantum sensing prototypes and as a building block for more complex state‑preparation pipelines.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

We construct exact circuits for $n$-qubit Dicke states of any Hamming weight $k$ using $O(n)$ single-qubit gates and CNOTs in $O(\log n)$ depth without ancillary qubits. The gate count and depth are asymptotically optimal for every $0<k<n$. We compress symmetric blocks to free output qubits for weight-dependent phases. A sign correction followed by a collective rotation produces a state whose overlap with the target is at least a positive constant, so exact amplitude amplification takes $O(1)$ steps. For balanced Dicke states ($k=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$), the logarithmic depth disproves the $Ω(k)$ depth lower bound conjectured by Yuan and Zhang (2025).
