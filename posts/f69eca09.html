NowSecure Finds No Detectable PQC Code in 97% of Mobile Apps

Source: The Quantum Insider October 20, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article shows that despite NIST’s progress on standardising post‑quantum algorithms, virtually all consumer mobile software is still using legacy cryptography, leaving billions of users potentially exposed once practical quantum attacks emerge. The finding highlights a stark lag between enterprise and cloud services, which are already piloting PQC, and the broader app ecosystem where developers have yet to integrate the new standards. It underscores the urgency for platform owners, SDK providers and regulators to push migration before quantum‑ready threats become realistic. The analysis may also undercount apps that embed custom or native crypto libraries, so the true adoption rate could be slightly higher but remains clearly minimal.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Insider Brief PRESS RELEASE — NowSecure today released an analysis, conducted with NowSecure Platform, of 136,711 iOS and Android apps. It found that 97% contain no detectable post-quantum cryptography (PQC), the new generation of encryption standards designed to withstand attacks from quantum computers.
