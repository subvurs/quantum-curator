QuantrolOx Raises €12 Million in Series A2

Source: The Quantum Insider October 27, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This €12 million Series A2 round signals growing confidence that QuantrolOx’s Oxford‑originated technology can bridge the gap between laboratory‑scale qubits and commercial quantum processors, a bottleneck many hardware firms are racing to solve. The backing from IW Capital, the European Innovation Council Fund and other venture partners aligns with Europe’s broader push to build an end‑to‑end quantum ecosystem, complementing recent large‑scale investments in cryogenic control and error‑correction platforms. If QuantrolOx can deliver scalable, low‑latency control hardware, it could accelerate time‑to‑market for a range of quantum applications while giving the continent a stronger foothold against US and Chinese rivals.

— Mark Eatherly
