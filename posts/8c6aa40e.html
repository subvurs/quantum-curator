Palm Beach State College and FAU Activate First Operational Segment of Florida’s Statewide Quantum-Safe Network

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 24, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks the first operational quantum‑safe link in Florida, demonstrating that commercial QKD hardware from IonQ can move beyond laboratory demos into a real campus‑to‑campus network. By anchoring the pilot in a defense‑funded corridor and pairing it with new academic programs, the project dovetails with national efforts to harden critical communications against future quantum attacks while cultivating a skilled workforce. The rollout also highlights practical hurdles—such as the need for dedicated fiber and high upfront costs—that will shape how quickly QKD can be scaled across broader telecom infrastructures.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Palm Beach State College (PBSC) and Florida Atlantic University (FAU) have activated Florida’s first operational quantum-safe network link, connecting their campuses using IonQ’s quantum key distribution (QKD) hardware. This initiative, supported by a $1 million defense infrastructure grant, is part of a larger plan to create a quantum-safe corridor across South Florida and develop a skilled workforce through PBSC's new Quantum Innovation Center and academic programs.
