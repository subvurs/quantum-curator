AI Commentary

This article matters because NIST’s new roadmap translates the abstract PQC standards into actionable steps for enterprises, cloud providers and government agencies that must protect data against tomorrow’s quantum attacks. By laying out migration timelines, algorithm selection guidance and interoperability checkpoints, it builds on the 2024 standard‑selection round and helps avoid the “cryptographic cliff” many security teams fear as quantum‑ready algorithms become mandatory. The roadmap also flags practical hurdles—such as performance trade‑offs and legacy system compatibility—that organizations will need to address before a smooth transition can be achieved.
