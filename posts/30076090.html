Lumen Expands Quantum Footprint with Ohio State Fiber Research and Fortinet PQC SASE Rollout

Source: Quantum Computing Report October 31, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because Lumen’s partnership with Ohio State tackles one of the biggest hurdles for quantum networking—demonstrating reliable qubit‑level transport over existing commercial fiber, a step that could accelerate the rollout of quantum‑enhanced backbones without costly new infrastructure. At the same time, embedding Fortinet’s post‑quantum cryptography into Lumen’s SASE platform shows how telecom operators are beginning to harden enterprise traffic against future attacks, aligning with the ongoing NIST PQC standardization effort. Together, these moves illustrate a rare convergence of research‑grade quantum transport and practical security solutions, signaling that the industry is moving from isolated labs toward integrated, market‑ready quantum services—though scaling the fiber experiments and validating PQC performance at scale remain open challenges.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Lumen Technologies is advancing quantum technology with two new initiatives. They are partnering with The Ohio State University to research quantum network transport over commercial optical fiber, and integrating Fortinet's post-quantum cryptography into their SASE solution to enhance enterprise security. These efforts aim to overcome challenges in quantum signal transmission and protect against future decryption threats.
