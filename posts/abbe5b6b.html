OptQC and NTT Strengthen Collaboration on Large-Scale Optical Quantum Computing

Source: The Quantum Insider August 03, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a rare convergence of a dedicated photonic‑hardware startup and Japan’s telecom heavyweight, signaling that the industry is now willing to back the ambitious goal of a fault‑tolerant optical quantum processor with on the order of one million qubits. By pairing OptQC’s integrated silicon‑photonic modules with NTT’s massive fiber‑network expertise, the partnership could accelerate the transition from laboratory‑scale boson‑sampling demos to scalable, error‑corrected architectures that complement recent advances from companies such as Xanadu and IBM’s photonic roadmap. While the alliance underscores growing confidence in photonics as a viable path to large‑scale quantum computing, practical deployment will still hinge on overcoming integration losses, cryogenic packaging, and the substantial overhead of optical error correction.

— Mark Eatherly
