NIELIT Hyderabad and QClairvoyance Train Over 200 Students in Quantum Technology

Source: The Quantum Insider November 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article highlights a growing emphasis on building a domestic talent pipeline as India accelerates its quantum roadmap, with NIELIT Hyderabad and QClairvoyance delivering hands‑on training to over 200 students from nine institutions. By exposing participants to emerging quantum communication protocols and application development, the workshop helps address the chronic shortage of skilled engineers that has long limited both academic research and commercial rollout in the region. The initiative dovetails with recent national efforts such as the Quantum Computing Mission and new funding for quantum startups, signaling that a broader ecosystem is being cultivated to translate theoretical advances into practical technologies. Readers should note that while the training is promising, sustained industry‑academia collaboration will be essential to turn this newly minted expertise into scalable quantum solutions.

— Mark Eatherly
