BTQ Technologies Brings MIMIQ Quantum Emulator to qBraid

Source: The Quantum Insider November 02, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it makes BTQ’s MIMIQ large‑scale quantum emulator—one of the most powerful classical simulators for near‑term devices—directly accessible through qBraid’s cloud‑based workflow, lowering the barrier for researchers to prototype and benchmark algorithms at sizes that would otherwise require costly hardware time. By integrating a high‑fidelity emulator with a popular orchestration platform, BTQ helps close the software–hardware gap that has slowed progress as quantum processors scale beyond 100 qubits, echoing recent moves by IBM and Google to provide robust simulation stacks alongside their chips. The practical upshot is that developers can now iterate faster, test error‑mitigation strategies, and validate performance before committing to real quantum hardware, accelerating the path toward useful quantum applications.

— Mark Eatherly
