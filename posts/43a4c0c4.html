NTT DOCOMO Deploys Second D-Wave Production Quantum Application to Optimize Telecom Network Signaling

Source: Quantum Computing Report August 18, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks the first time a major carrier has moved beyond pilot studies to run two back‑to‑back, production‑grade quantum workloads on D‑Wave’s hybrid annealing system, showing that quantum‑enhanced optimization can be integrated into live telecom operations. By slashing peak location‑registration signaling by more than 65 % and cutting paging load, the TA‑List optimizer demonstrates a concrete cost‑saving and capacity‑boosting use case that complements recent industry experiments in logistics, finance and materials design. The success also underscores the niche where quantum annealing excels—large combinatorial scheduling problems—but it remains tied to D‑Wave’s specific hardware and cloud service, so broader adoption will depend on expanding algorithmic libraries and proving similar gains across other network functions.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

Japanese telecommunications operator NTT DOCOMO has deployed its second commercial, production-grade quantum application powered by D-Wave Quantum Inc. (NASDAQ: QBTS).
