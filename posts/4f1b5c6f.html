LUMI AI Factory Selects IQM’s Halocene Roadmap for Europe’s First Superconducting Logical Qubit System

Source: Quantum Computing Report September 11, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article marks a milestone for Europe’s quantum ecosystem by announcing the first superconducting system designed from the ground up to host fault‑tolerant logical qubits, moving beyond prototype demonstrations toward scalable error‑corrected computing. By embedding IQM’s Halocene roadmap into the LUMI AI Factory, the project creates a true hybrid supercomputing stack that can offload workloads between classical HPC, AI accelerators, and quantum processors—a model other regions are racing to replicate. While the phased rollout through 2029 means practical applications will still be years away, the commitment of EuroHPC funding signals strong institutional backing and could accelerate the transition from noisy‑intermediate‑scale devices to usable quantum advantage in chemistry, optimization, and materials science.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

IQM Quantum Computers will deploy Europe's first logical-qubit-capable superconducting quantum computer, LUMI-IQ, at CSC's Kajaani, Finland data center. This system, co-funded by the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking, will be integrated into the LUMI AI Factory, creating a hybrid HPC-AI-quantum supercomputing platform. LUMI-IQ will be delivered in three phases through 2029, starting with 150 physical qubits and eventually supporting up to 9 fault-tolerant logical qubits for advanced quantum computations.
