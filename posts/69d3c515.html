Readout-Induced Degradation of Transmon Lifetimes: Interplay of TLSs and Qubit Spectral Reshaping

Source: PRX Quantum September 17, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article demonstrates that the frequency of the read‑out drive can dramatically shorten transmon lifetimes by resonantly activating spurious two‑level systems, a decoherence channel that has been largely overlooked in most qubit‑characterisation protocols. By combining experiment, theory and large‑scale simulations, the authors provide concrete design rules for shaping the qubit spectrum and choosing read‑out tones that suppress this TLS‑mediated loss—a timely advance as superconducting processors scale up and measurement overhead becomes a dominant error source. The findings complement recent efforts to engineer materials and device geometries for reduced TLS density, but practical implementation will still need to balance these constraints against multiplexed read‑out bandwidth and circuit complexity.

— Mark Eatherly
