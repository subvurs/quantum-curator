Brian Gaucher (ERVA): Why engineering, not physics, now limits quantum progress

Source: The Quantum Insider September 05, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This interview spotlights a pivotal shift in the quantum race: with qubit physics largely mature, the bottleneck is now turning experimental prototypes into reproducible, large‑scale hardware—a challenge that sits squarely in engineering. Gaucher’s perspective aligns with recent industry moves toward standardized fabrication processes and supply‑chain development, underscoring why companies are investing heavily in design‑for‑manufacturability and reliability testing. Readers should watch this trend closely, as breakthroughs in quantum engineering will determine how quickly practical quantum advantage can be delivered beyond the lab.

— Mark Eatherly
