China Tests Quantum Technology in Power Grid

Source: The Quantum Insider August 18, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because it marks one of the first large‑scale field deployments of quantum sensors, secure communications and even prototype quantum computing hardware inside an operational power substation, showing that the technology is moving from laboratory proof‑of‑concept to real‑world infrastructure. It builds on a wave of national initiatives—such as the U.S. Department of Energy’s grid‑monitoring quantum network and Europe’s pilot quantum‑enhanced metrology projects—by demonstrating how domestically produced devices can be integrated across dozens of functional categories in a live 220 kV system. If the trials prove reliable, utilities could gain unprecedented fault detection precision and cyber‑resilience, though scaling up will still require solving integration, cost and standards challenges.

— Mark Eatherly
