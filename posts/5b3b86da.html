NQCC Signs Enterprise Agreement with Phasecraft to Provide Quantum Software via SparQ Access

Source: Quantum Computing Report November 06, 2026 Curated by Mark Eatherly

Curator's Take

AI Commentary

This article matters because the NQCC‑Phasecraft deal creates a streamlined pathway for UK enterprises to experiment with quantum software—Magritte, Monet and Mondrian—through the SparQ Access platform, directly addressing the “software‑first” bottleneck that has slowed real‑world quantum advantage assessments. By issuing an open call for proposals and onboarding its first cohort in spring 2027, the partnership mirrors other national initiatives (e.g., IBM’s Q Network, AWS Braket) that aim to democratise access while providing hands‑on support for integration into existing workflows. The move could accelerate proof‑of‑concept projects across finance, materials and logistics, though actual gains will still hinge on the maturity of underlying quantum hardware.

— Mark Eatherly

Summary

In November 2026, the NQCC and Phasecraft signed an agreement to provide UK organizations with access to Phasecraft's quantum software via SparQ Access. This partnership aims to lower the technical barrier for evaluating quantum advantage in various fields. An open call for proposals will be issued in late November 2026, with the first user cohort starting in Spring 2027, receiving support to deploy Magritte, Monet, and Mondrian software platforms.
