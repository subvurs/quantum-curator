Grow, Compress, and Perturb: A Hybrid Quantum-Classical Pathway to Scalable Quantum Chemistry on Quantum Computers

While Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) variants are among leading candidates for electronic structure calculations on quantum computers, gradient-based operator selection limits their scalability. To solve this, we introduce the Hamiltonian-gradient-free VULCAN (Variational Upbuilding, Layered ...

This article introduces VULCAN, a gradient‑free, layered VQE framework that slashes the operator pool size by up to an order of magnitude and eliminates costly Hamiltonian‑gradient measurements, directly tackling one of the biggest bottlenecks in scalable quantum chemistry. By coupling a grow‑then‑compress ansatz construction with a stochastic Epstein‑Nesbet perturbative correction, the authors demonstrate chemical accuracy on systems as large as 32 logical qubits while using only a fraction of the measurement budget required by ADAPT‑VQE. If these techniques translate to near‑term hardware, they could accelerate the path toward practical quantum simulations of medium‑size molecules without prohibitive circuit depth or sampling overhead.
