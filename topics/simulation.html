Neural Network Decoders For Qubit Loss

Qubit loss is a significant source of error in many quantum hardware platforms, requiring circuits that periodically replace or reset qubits to maintain fault-tolerance. When the reset process includes loss-resolving measurements, this information can improve decoding and substantially reduce overhe...

This article shows that a convolutional‑neural‑network decoder can ingest loss‑resolution data and recover the same logical error suppression as costly maximum‑likelihood methods, opening a practical path to fault‑tolerant operation on platforms where qubit loss dominates. By introducing the high‑throughput “slim” sampler, the authors extend loss‑aware decoding beyond surface codes to a 144‑qubit qLDPC bicycle code, demonstrating over three orders of magnitude improvement in logical error rates at sub‑percent physical error rates. The work bridges the gap between machine‑learning‑based decoders and realistic hardware constraints, suggesting that near‑term quantum processors could achieve much lower overheads without redesigning their error‑correction circuitry. However, the results still rely on simulated Pauli‑plus‑loss noise models, so experimental validation will be essential to confirm scalability on actual devices.
