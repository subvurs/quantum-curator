Insider Brief PRESS RELEASE — IBM (NYSE: IBM), the University of Chicago, the State of Illinois, and Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park (IQMP) today unveiled an IBM Quantum System Two in Chicago, IBM’s first quantum computer in Illinois and the company’s most advanced system available. Initi...

This article marks IBM’s first deployment of its next‑generation Quantum System Two outside the company’s traditional data‑center sites, bringing a 433‑qubit processor to Chicago’s Hyde Park Labs and linking it directly with the University of Chicago and the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park. By expanding high‑performance quantum access to a major academic and industrial hub, IBM accelerates the regional talent pipeline and gives researchers immediate hands‑on experience with the hardware that underpins its 2025 roadmap toward fault‑tolerant machines. While still a noisy intermediate‑scale device, the system’s larger qubit count and improved connectivity promise more complex algorithmic demonstrations and faster iteration on error‑mitigation techniques for near‑term applications.
