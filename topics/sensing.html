Bell-pair distillation under imperfect operations

We recently proposed an entanglement distillation protocol based on local operations and classical communication that probabilistically and universally distills a maximally entangled Bell pair from multiple copies of an unknown two-qubit state [Phys. Rev. Lett. 135, 260202 (2025)]. Here, we investig...

This article shows that the Bell‑pair distillation protocol introduced last year can still boost entanglement fidelity even when the underlying gates and measurements are noisy, a crucial step toward deploying entanglement purification on near‑term hardware. By mapping out which incoherent versus coherent error regimes preserve the advantage—and revealing how asymmetric errors can be balanced through complementary postselection—the work links theoretical guarantees to realistic quantum‑network nodes and sensing platforms. The findings suggest that modest‑quality devices could already benefit from built‑in entanglement cleaning, though the protocol’s tolerance varies sharply with error type, underscoring the need for tailored calibration in practical deployments.
