Riverlane’s data‑driven augmentation of Detector Error Models builds on recent machine‑learning‑assisted error‑characterisation efforts, delivering up to a 75 % logical‑error reduction in simulation and a measurable 14 % gain on actual hardware—an encouraging step toward scalable fault tolerance. The results also highlight that while the method scales well in silico, translating those gains fully to noisy quantum processors will require further integration with device‑specific calibration cycles.
